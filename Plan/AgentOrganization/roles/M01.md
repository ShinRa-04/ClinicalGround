# M01 — Lead care manager / case manager

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/M01.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Coordinate a patient-centered plan and track unresolved needs.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

What unresolved patient goal or service obligation still has a named owner?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Candidate workflow-owner function when the actual task assigns it; retain explicit role modes and observed completion obligations.

One case owner can combine coordination functions while retaining evidence and professional boundaries.

Primary grouping: [W13 — Care coordination and follow-through](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w13). Maintain goals, assessment context, referrals, outreach, barriers and observed follow-through across care transitions.

## Can its work be merged?

A case owner can combine administrative coordination, navigation and general education support while invoking specific expertise when needed.

**Boundary that must survive:** Clinical assessment permissions, patient statements, discharge authority and professional education scope remain explicit; coordination is not physical service delivery.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Patient preferences and assessment; Clinical plan and service responses.

Potential work products from that model: Shared coordination plan; Assigned follow-up and unresolved-need record.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A plan exists but outreach, referral response or follow-up is never completed.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** A merged worker loses patient dialogue state, invents assessment answers, or marks a referral as completed care.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

These examples do not establish national prevalence. Coordination title is not a license or independent prescribing credential; unavailable R23 is not needed to establish these examples.

**Prior applicability:** Credential may be nursing, social work or another background; separate coordination authority from that credential. Published staffing examples differ; this is not evidence of national role prevalence.
