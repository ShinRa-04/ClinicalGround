# U09 — Determination / notification coordinator

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/U09.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Deliver the authorized outcome with accurate procedural information.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Does the notice exactly correspond to the stored decision, version and recipient?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Candidate controlled operation derived from authoritative state, not an open-ended decision-making persona.

Rendering and delivery can merge into a workflow owner; neither may invent an outcome.

Primary grouping: [W10 — Notice preparation and delivery](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w10). Render and deliver the observed authorized decision with the appropriate reason and review information.

## Can its work be merged?

This can be a controlled operation of the payer workflow owner rather than another open-ended agent.

**Boundary that must survive:** The notice must derive from the authoritative decision/version; drafting or delivery must not change the substantive decision.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Authorized determination and rationale; Recipient and route information.

Potential work products from that model: Decision notice; Dispatch/communication record.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A generated notice changes the reason or outcome without updating authority/state.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** Notice text contradicts the stored decision, wrong recipient is used, or draft is recorded as delivered.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Receipt, decision, dispatch and implementation are separate events. Actual timing/contents must be resolved for the route.

**Prior applicability:** Contents, recipients and deadlines depend on the actual product/process; no universal time limit is encoded here.
