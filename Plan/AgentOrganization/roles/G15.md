# G15 — Billing / patient accounts / claim-denial worker

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/G15.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Process payment/claim work and explain or correct administrative account issues.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Which payment/claim event is actually in scope and observed?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Coordinate the service through permitted interfaces; require observed acceptance/fulfillment rather than an internal persona’s promise.

Account handling can share documents with authorization while preserving distinct financial state.

Primary grouping: [W23 — Billing and post-service account interface](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w23). Track account/claim status and administrative payment questions when actually in scope.

## Can its work be merged?

Share identifiers and authorized documents with access/dispute workflows; invoke payment reasoning only where needed.

**Boundary that must survive:** Prior authorization, benefit verification, claim acceptance, adjudication and payment are different states.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Service/coding record; Benefit and claim response.

Potential work products from that model: Claim/account record; Correction or payment-dispute follow-up.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** Authorization or coverage verification is reported as a paid claim.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** Authorization success is reported as a paid claim or a completed financial outcome without evidence.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Pre-service authorization, claim adjudication, financial assistance and patient liability are different decisions.

**Prior applicability:** Downstream claim adjudication and pre-service authorization are distinct; exact payment rules are outside a generic role title.
