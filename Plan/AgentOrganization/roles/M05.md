# M05 — Pharmacy technician / medication-access coordinator

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/M05.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Support medication operations and access logistics.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

What access/fulfillment information is missing without requiring clinical medication decisions?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Candidate explicit mode of a shared workflow owner, conditional on scope and measured context/coordination behavior.

Technician/access operations can share medication state but not clinical permissions.

Primary grouping: [W15 — Medication review and access](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w15). Distinguish medication expertise, existing therapy instructions and administrative access/fulfillment work.

## Can its work be merged?

Share a medication evidence record and access workflow, while separating clinical proposals from technician/access operations.

**Boundary that must survive:** Protocol-authorized pharmacist activity, general prescribing and technician tasks are not equivalent; local authority is unresolved.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Prescription and patient/benefit details; Pharmacist/prescriber instructions.

Potential work products from that model: Processed access/refill task; Status and clinical clarification request.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A stock or authorization issue is resolved by changing the medication.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** Medication availability is treated as a treatment order or access staff alter therapy.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

An access assignment does not replace pharmacist/prescriber judgment. R09 historical examples are not proof of current staffing or procedure.

**Prior applicability:** Technician permissions vary; medication-access work can be an advanced assignment but remains distinct from pharmacist judgment.
