# M28 — Hospice interdisciplinary group and attending functions (distinct roles)

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/M28.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Coordinate hospice care while distinguishing interdisciplinary, RN coordination, attending and certifying/admission functions.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Which IDG, RN coordination, attending, certification or admission function is needed?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Decompose the profile’s multiple functions before assigning worker identities or decision ownership.

The existing composite profile is a warning against one-role/one-agent mapping.

Primary grouping: [W18 — Hospice and spiritual-care interfaces](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w18). Coordinate the appropriate hospice group/attending and spiritual support contributions.

## Can its work be merged?

Common communication plumbing may connect participants; the composite hospice profile must be decomposed into functions before creating worker identities.

**Boundary that must survive:** IDG planning, designated RN coordination, attending, certification/admission and spiritual support are distinct; the cited hospice evidence is the 2025 edition.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Hospice referral/context and goals; Professional assessments.

Potential work products from that model: Interdisciplinary care plan; Patient/family support and change communication.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A single hospice worker is assigned every signatory function from a group description.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** One hospice agent is assigned all professional decisions or spiritual support is treated as clinical certification.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

The IDG is a group, not one individual. Evidence is expressly the October 2025 codification; complete current 2026 hospice-rule verification is not claimed.

**Prior applicability:** V11, V16 and V17 are the official October 2025 codification, not a complete current-2026 update check. They distinguish IDG/designated RN coordination, physician certification and admission. A patient-designated attending may have a different allowed credential; that does not grant every attending terminal-illness certification authority.
