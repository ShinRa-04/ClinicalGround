# M21 — Home-health aide / personal-care worker

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/M21.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Provide assigned personal support and report relevant changes.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

What evidence shows personal-care work actually occurred rather than being arranged?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Coordinate the service through permitted interfaces; require observed acceptance/fulfillment rather than an internal persona’s promise.

A physical service worker is not an LLM reasoning role.

Primary grouping: [W17 — Receiving services and real-world fulfillment](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w17). Coordinate service acceptance, availability, scheduling and observed delivery/attendance evidence.

## Can its work be merged?

A shared logistics coordinator can manage requests; providers, workers and actual delivery remain distinct external parties/events.

**Boundary that must survive:** Referral sent, accepted, scheduled, delivered and completed are separate states. Capacity and actual attendance must come from allowed observations.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Assigned plan and instructions; Patient needs/preferences.

Potential work products from that model: Completed-task record; Observed changes and unmet-support report.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A scheduled aide visit is represented as delivered care.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** A dispatcher persona or optimistic plan is used as evidence that a person received care, transport, food or equipment.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Do not apply Medicare coverage conditions to every private aide or treat personal care as equivalent to skilled nursing.

**Prior applicability:** Health-related tasks depend on state, training and supervision; personal support is not equivalent to skilled nursing.
