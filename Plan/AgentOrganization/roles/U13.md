# U13 — Medical-necessity appeal / reconsideration physician

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/U13.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Make the applicable reconsideration judgment independently of the initial decision-making.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

How is uninvolved review established beyond using a different prompt?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Preserve applicable uninvolved-review accountability; a fresh model context is only an internal error-checking experiment.

The cited human independence/expertise rule is not satisfied by naming an LLM physician.

Primary grouping: [W11 — Internal appeal administration and reconsideration](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w11). Establish standing/route, assemble the appeal record and obtain the applicable reconsideration outcome.

## Can its work be merged?

Filing support may share software, but do not collapse initial-decision ownership and a required uninvolved reconsideration function.

**Boundary that must survive:** For the cited MA rule, actual uninvolved persons and appropriate physician expertise are requirements; a fresh LLM context is not proof of those human qualifications.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Appeal case and initial rationale; Relevant record and criteria.

Potential work products from that model: Reconsidered determination; Rationale and onward-review record when required.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** The initial author changes its persona and self-certifies compliant reconsideration.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** The initial author self-certifies independent reconsideration merely by changing its role label.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

Independence is not merely seniority. This card does not establish the credentials/delegation of any actual reviewer.
