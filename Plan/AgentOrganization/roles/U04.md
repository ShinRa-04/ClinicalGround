# U04 — Payer physician reviewer / medical director acting on a case

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/U04.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Perform assigned clinical coverage review and document the rationale.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Which case decision is authorized, and is this person/function initial reviewer or reconsiderer?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Keep a distinct review-stage/permission contract; test separate reasoning context if it improves a defined review task.

Expert review does not supply independence from an earlier determination.

Primary grouping: [W09 — Initial review and specialty reasoning](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w09). Evaluate applicable criteria and evidence, request needed specialty input, and propose only authorized case actions.

## Can its work be merged?

Shared policy/evidence machinery can serve distinct review modes; separate bounded reasoning calls may be useful for genuinely different questions.

**Boundary that must survive:** Professional expertise, delegation, recommendation versus determination, and actual signoff remain separate. R13 signoff tension is unresolved; delegation may exclude appeals.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Review record and criteria; Escalation question.

Potential work products from that model: Clinical coverage rationale; Determination or request for further evidence.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A medical-director title is used to make a self-review independent.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** A shared review mode crosses product/delegation limits, hides conflicting evidence or requires more independent expertise than its input supports.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Initial review, final institutional accountability and independent reconsideration are separate; medical director is not a universal superior over the treating clinician.

**Prior applicability:** Case-review function is distinct from organization-wide CMO governance; authority depends on product, license, expertise and delegation. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.
