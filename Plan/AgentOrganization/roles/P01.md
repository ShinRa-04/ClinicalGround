# P01 — Patient / insured member

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/P01.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Express personal goals and make applicable informed choices about participation and care.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Whose expressed preference is being recorded, and what observation establishes it?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Keep a separately attributed participant/evidence interface when this information belongs to an external party; use an acting role mode only where the actual task explicitly assigns it.

Patient answers must be observed; a simulated internal patient must never supply the benchmark patient response.

Primary grouping: [W01 — Patient and support participants](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w01). Obtain goals, observations and applicable representation facts through permitted interaction.

## Can its work be merged?

Represent related participants in one case model, with separate identities and statement attribution; do not replace them with a worker that supplies convenient answers.

**Boundary that must survive:** Patient choice, caregiver observation and lawful representation are different facts. Family relationship does not establish decision authority.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Understandable clinical explanation; Options and relevant coverage information.

Potential work products from that model: Preferences and reported history; Consent/refusal or request for clarification.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A coordinator writes consent because its own patient persona agreed.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** A merged record attributes caregiver wishes to the patient or treats missing consent as agreement.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Patient rights do not create entitlement to inappropriate treatment; capacity, minors and emergency rules require case-specific law.

**Prior applicability:** Capacity, minors, emergencies and surrogate authority require applicable law and case facts; this is not a blanket consent rule.
