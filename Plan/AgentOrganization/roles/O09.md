# O09 — Financial counselor / assistance specialist

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/O09.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Help the patient understand costs and available assistance options.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Which affordability barrier and assistance option are actually supported?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Candidate explicit mode of a shared workflow owner, conditional on scope and measured context/coordination behavior.

Financial coordination can merge with access work; eligibility/awards remain observed outcomes.

Primary grouping: [W05 — Provider access and authorization operations](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w05). Identify the case, check administrative requirements, assemble supported materials, submit, track and coordinate scheduling/access barriers.

## Can its work be merged?

These operations can share a case record and an accountable workflow owner; combining them may reduce summary handoffs.

**Boundary that must survive:** Identity, coverage verification, authorization outcome, financial estimate and service booking are different states. Management rank does not confer clinical power.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Patient financial concerns; Benefits/cost information and program criteria.

Potential work products from that model: Cost explanation/estimate; Assistance application or referral.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** An assistance application is reported as approved funding.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** Measured merged execution repeatedly confuses administrative states, loses obligations or lacks genuinely different access scopes.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

The counselor does not automatically decide assistance eligibility, benefit coverage or final claim payment.

**Prior applicability:** Estimates, assistance approvals, authorization and final claims are separate records and decisions.
