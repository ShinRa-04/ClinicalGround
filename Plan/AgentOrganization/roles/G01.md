# G01 — Provider medical director / chief medical officer

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/G01.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Provide assigned clinical-program leadership in the provider organization.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Is this a provider governance mandate or a case-level treating/review function?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Retain the approved mandate/version and external approval identity; retrieval/analysis may be shared without creating governance authority.

A leadership title is not authority over payer determinations.

Primary grouping: [W20 — Policy, legal and clinical governance](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w20). Establish applicable policies, approved versions and unresolved governance/legal questions.

## Can its work be merged?

Share policy retrieval and change tracking; proposal, clinical governance, committee approval and legal advice remain separately attributed functions.

**Boundary that must survive:** An analyst draft or agent-generated rule is not approved policy. Provider and payer leaders have different mandates; hierarchy is not global authority.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Clinical quality/escalation findings; Applicable professional and organizational requirements.

Potential work products from that model: Clinical-program policy/escalation response; Improvement direction.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** Provider leadership is modeled as the payer reviewer’s boss.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** A task worker rewrites the controlling rule or approves its own policy revision to make a case pass.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Exact authority over practitioners requires bylaws, privileges and assignment; the title does not establish a physician-over-all-professions chain.

**Prior applicability:** Actual reporting lines, medical-staff authority and privileges require local bylaws/organization chart; no universal CMO-boss graph is asserted.

Source IDs in the prior dossier: B22, R14, A15. These support the scoped healthcare context, not the new software partition. See the prior [source provenance](../../HealthcareRoles/SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md) for failed/dated/example evidence.

Related authority questions: Apply the explicit package/role boundaries; no additional decision power is inferred..
