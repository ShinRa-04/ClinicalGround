# O10 — Provider denial / appeal specialist

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/O10.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Organize the provider response to an adverse outcome.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Which dispute route preserves the correct issue, standing and filing status?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Candidate workflow-owner function when the actual task assigns it; retain explicit role modes and observed completion obligations.

Administrative dispute preparation can be shared without impersonating clinical peer review.

Primary grouping: [W07 — Provider dispute preparation and clinical exchange](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w07). Assemble the correct challenge and preserve clinical contributions and process status.

## Can its work be merged?

Administrative filing preparation and evidence collection may share a workflow; clinical peer participation remains an explicitly authorized interface.

**Boundary that must survive:** Peer discussion is not automatically formal appeal; filing, clinical argument, appointment, deadline and acceptance are separate.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Adverse notice; Request/record and representation information.

Potential work products from that model: Appeal/correction packet; Submission tracking and unresolved issues.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A complaint or peer discussion is treated as a filed formal appeal.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** A merged job claims a successful appeal from a peer-discussion note or submits an unattributed clinical opinion.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Standing and representation need separate verification; a billing/appeal job title is not a patient appointment.

**Prior applicability:** Separate pre-service authorization disputes from post-service claim denials; actual notice determines route and deadlines.
