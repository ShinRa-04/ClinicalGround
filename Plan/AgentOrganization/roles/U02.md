# U02 — Payer eligibility / benefit specialist

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/U02.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Apply the plan's administrative membership and benefit information.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Which product/eligibility facts authorize this particular administrative outcome?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Candidate explicit mode of a shared workflow owner, conditional on scope and measured context/coordination behavior.

Benefit-only and medical-necessity conclusions remain distinct.

Primary grouping: [W08 — Payer intake, benefits and status operations](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w08). Establish administrative context, collect information, route work and communicate observed status.

## Can its work be merged?

A common payer case owner can combine collection and service/status functions with role-scoped operations.

**Boundary that must survive:** Limited delegated nonclinical decisions are program-specific; medical-necessity denial and general benefits/status work remain different permissions.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Enrollment/benefit record; Requested service details.

Potential work products from that model: Benefit/eligibility finding; Administrative explanation or referral.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A coverage mismatch becomes an unsupported clinical denial.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** Status communication becomes an invented determination, or a clinically complete review is forced after a valid administrative terminal path.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

A benefits title alone does not establish signatory power. Administrative coverage exclusions and clinical medical necessity need different reasoning/review.

**Prior applicability:** Exact benefit authority and decision notice are plan-specific; not every benefits specialist is authorized to issue a denial. R13 permits some assigned benefit-only decisions; actual signatory authority still requires the plan/delegation.
