# M02 — Medical social worker (credential-dependent practice)

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/M02.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Assess psychosocial and practical support needs and connect appropriate resources.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

What social-work function and credential level are actually relevant?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Candidate on-demand specialist with a specific question and bounded evidence view; proposal by default, not assumed case-finalization authority.

Clinical job wording may include supervised pre-licensure work; title is not independent licensure.

Primary grouping: [W14 — Psychosocial and behavioral-health interfaces](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w14). Identify psychosocial needs and interpret or request appropriate specialist contributions.

## Can its work be merged?

Common intake/resource-finding support can be shared; separate expertise and restricted information views may be necessary for specialist reasoning.

**Boundary that must survive:** Job title, independent clinical licensure and additional prescribing certification differ. Do not collapse social support, therapy and prescribing.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Patient goals and social assessment; Available resources.

Potential work products from that model: Psychosocial/resource assessment; Referral and follow-up.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A generic social-work persona supplies unrestricted psychotherapy authority.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** A generic behavioral-health persona acts beyond its assigned function or exposes protected material to unrelated work.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

UCLA describes an unlicensed Clinical Social Worker II role. A job title containing clinical does not establish independent clinical licensure.

**Prior applicability:** Job titles and licensure differ; distinguish supportive casework from licensed clinical treatment. UCLA describes a pre-licensure Clinical Social Worker II assignment; clinical job wording does not establish independent clinical licensure.
