# C01 — Ordering / treating physician

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/C01.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Assess the clinical problem and recommend or order appropriate care.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Which clinical facts/orders were actually authored by the treating professional?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Keep a separately attributed participant/evidence interface when this information belongs to an external party; use an acting role mode only where the actual task explicitly assigns it.

The solver may organize evidence without impersonating the author.

Primary grouping: [W04 — Treating-team and clinical-evidence interfaces](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w04). Request and attribute existing clinical assessments, orders, referral context and externally produced evidence.

## Can its work be merged?

A shared evidence-request adapter may serve these contacts; that is an interface merger, not one interchangeable clinical occupation.

**Boundary that must survive:** Credential, jurisdiction, privileges, authorship and treating relationship remain distinct; reading a chart is not performing treatment or acquiring prescribing authority.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Patient history and examination; Results and specialist information.

Potential work products from that model: Authenticated clinical note/order; Clinical rationale and instructions.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A stronger medical rationale is invented and attributed to the physician.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** The harness fabricates a clinician statement or treats every clinical title as equivalent signatory permission.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Clinical recommendation, patient participation and coverage are separate functions; privileges and jurisdiction remain local facts.

**Prior applicability:** License, privileges and clinical setting control the activity; this is not a hierarchy over insurer employees.
