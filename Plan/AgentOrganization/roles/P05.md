# P05 — Healthcare interpreter / communication-access professional

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/P05.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Enable accurate spoken or signed communication.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Does the interpreter preserve speaker attribution and unanswered questions?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Prefer a bounded language/communication capability; retain speaker, content and understanding distinctions.

Interpretation supports the conversation; it cannot create clinical answers.

Primary grouping: [W03 — Language and communication support](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w03). Support oral/written communication without changing the substantive author or decision owner.

## Can its work be merged?

One language service can expose separate interpretation and translation operations; a persistent autonomous persona is not inherently needed.

**Boundary that must survive:** Written fidelity, oral turn-taking, participant comprehension and substantive consent must remain distinguishable. Qualification is not proved by a language prompt.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Speaker's actual message; Communication/language needs.

Potential work products from that model: Interpreted message; Clarification or access concern.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** An interpreter fills a missing assessment answer to keep dialogue moving.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** Translation drops a negation, invents an answer, or is recorded as informed understanding without evidence.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

Qualification, language and communication needs matter; no independent medical decision is conferred by interpreting.

**Prior applicability:** Qualification and access arrangements must fit the communication need; interpreting is distinct from written translation.
