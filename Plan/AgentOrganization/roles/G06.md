## What function are we trying to preserve?

Identify and help correct recurring process/quality problems.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

Is this an internal quality check, an independent review or the official benchmark result?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Primarily a development/quality-analysis function unless a task explicitly requires it at runtime; keep official grading separate.

A bounded critic can aid quality without becoming an adjudicator or hidden verifier.

Primary grouping: [W21 — Management, quality, analytics and learning](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w21). Manage process design, training, workload and aggregate quality analysis outside ordinary case execution.

## Can its work be merged?

A design-time analysis service may cover several functions, while runtime checks remain bounded and separate from official grading.

**Boundary that must survive:** Operational management is not clinical or appeal authority; internal diagnostics must not read hidden verifier answers or masquerade as official scores.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Authorized operational/clinical data; Audit criteria.

Potential work products from that model: Quality findings; Improvement recommendations and follow-up.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A model’s own success declaration is reported as official pass evidence.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** Training feedback contaminates held-out evaluation, or a self-audit is reported as independent official success.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

A finding is not a patient diagnosis, automatic coverage reversal or causal proof of improved outcomes.

**Prior applicability:** Audit access and methods must be authorized; observational findings are not automatically causal evidence.
