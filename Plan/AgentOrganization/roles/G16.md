# G16 — Regulator / oversight official

[Role analysis index](../ROLE_ANALYSIS.md) · [Audited healthcare dossier](../../HealthcareRoles/roles/G16.md)

**Status:** proposed design inference; actual task assignment and runtime benefit are unverified. The task/tool contract can assign a simulated function even where the analogous human job needs credentials; do not invent extra real-person steps.

## What function are we trying to preserve?

Exercise the specific public oversight authority assigned by law.

The linked healthcare dossier contains the responsibilities, scoped decisions and evidence. This analysis does not upgrade its conditions or model contacts into universal facts.

## Specific question

What oversight power and case route are actually invoked?

## Provisional conclusion and reason

Preserve the actual external forum, route and observed outcome; an internal critic is a different function.

A regulator is not a universal super-agent or ordinary appeal endpoint.

Primary grouping: [W12 — External review and adjudication interfaces](../WORK_PACKAGES.md#w12). Identify the applicable external route, transmit authorized material, and observe externally issued outcomes.

## Can its work be merged?

A routing adapter can share transport/record plumbing, while preserving distinct institutions, jurisdiction, eligibility and decision authority.

**Boundary that must survive:** MA IRE, commercial IRO, regulator, ALJ, Council and court are not one escalating internal worker. External decisions cannot be invented by a local reviewer persona.

A common work package is not proof that one employee may perform every activity, or that one persistent LLM is best. A shared adapter, a role mode and a separate worker are all alternatives.

## Information and output contract

Context from the prior proposed model: Applicable oversight issue; Authorized complaint/evidence.

Potential work products from that model: Oversight disposition or required action; Referral to proper forum.

For a software invocation, add case/stage, source versions, explicit unknowns, allowed operations, requested output, current owner and stop condition. These are proposed internal fields, not verified API parameters or mandatory institutional forms.

## Counterexample and reconsideration

**Failure to detect:** A local model manufactures a regulatory ruling to resolve disagreement.

**Reason to split/revise the package:** A harness creates a binding external reversal or moves automatically through every forum without eligibility.

Test a mode-based owner and a bounded specialist/persistent-worker alternative against the same task-visible facts and declared resource constraint; preserve source, authority and completion distinctions. No such comparison has run.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence

A complaint may trigger oversight or referral rather than a coverage reversal; identify jurisdiction and legal process.

**Prior applicability:** Identify agency, program and legal route; no blanket authority is assigned to every regulator or ombudsman.
