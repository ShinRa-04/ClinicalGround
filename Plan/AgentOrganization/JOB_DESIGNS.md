# Worked job designs and their alternatives

These proposals show how the role analysis could shape **our agentic harness**. They are not deployed jobs, verified tool permissions or measured improvements. Names describe functional responsibilities, not a claim that the software is a licensed person. Each design must first be mapped to the actual assigned instruction and tool interface.

## JD01 — Provider authorization case owner

**Functions considered together:** O01 packet/submission tracking, O02 referral coordination, O03 identity checks, O04 benefits/requirements, O05 scheduling, O09 affordability coordination and O12 process escalation. This is the W05 package. It does not imply every case needs all seven functions.

**Question:** can a single owner maintain continuity across identifiers, supported materials, requests and response handling more reliably than repeated worker handoffs?

**Inputs:** actual instruction, case identity, permitted clinical evidence, observed coverage/requirement information, current request status, actual correspondence and unresolved obligations. Clinical facts remain attributed to their source; unavailable evidence is recorded as missing.

**Proposed responsibilities:** identify the relevant case; determine the actual stage; obtain permitted evidence; assemble supported materials; execute only the required submission step; observe receipt and response; handle the actual information/dispute route if assigned; coordinate downstream scheduling/access tasks only where in scope.

**Outputs:** supported packet/draft, observed submission/response evidence, outstanding information requests, current authoritative status and named next obligation. A packet is not a receipt, a receipt is not authorization, and scheduling is not care delivered. These are internal distinctions, not a declaration of the exact simulator’s status names.

**Helper alternatives:** a bounded record/coding consistency capability (W06), a policy/evidence specialist, or a separate packet-preparation worker. Each returns evidence references, missing facts and a proposal. The owner checks case/version before using it. Treating authors and actual external evidence remain interfaces; a helper cannot invent a stronger clinician statement.

**Three organizational options:**

| Option | Benefit to investigate | Main failure to challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One owner with explicit modes | Keeps identifiers, versions and follow-up in one context | Context interference or omitted identity/benefit distinctions |
| Owner plus bounded evidence helper | Gives focused review without a second writer | Helper returns unsupported or stale evidence |
| Persistent preparation/submission/follow-up workers | Sustained specialization and clearer local responsibilities | Lost handoff obligations, duplicate submissions, extra messages |

**Provisional conclusion:** this is a plausible merged job to test. Supporting reasons are shared case context and continuity obligations, not proof of a legal or performance result. The functions should separate if actual tools require incompatible principals/views, repeated context errors remain, or competent role-worker trials do better under the declared resource constraint. Relevant decisions: AD04/AD07/AD08; stress cases T01/T02/T06/T15/T16.

## JD02 — Payer case operations with explicit review stages

**Functions considered together:** U01 intake, U02 benefit context, U10/U11 service communication, U17 operational routing and a controlled U09 notice operation. W09 review functions are an explicit logical stage; specialty reasoning can be a capability call or separate worker. W11/W12 reconsideration/external processes retain their own scope and provenance.

**Question:** which boundaries require a separate reasoning context or accountable actor, and which can remain named stages in one owner’s execution?

**Inputs:** actual product/task, administrative facts, current case/stage, permitted clinical evidence, applicable policy/version, prior review contributions and observed outcomes. Do not infer universal signing authority from a title or committee rank. R13’s prior signoff ambiguity remains unresolved; it is not settled by the grouping.

**Proposed responsibilities:** collect and route; distinguish administrative and clinical questions; invoke only applicable reviews; preserve the difference between consultation, recommendation and authorized determination; prepare/deliver the corresponding notice through actual tools; track any required review/implementation state.

**Important branch:** the pinned architecture describes a failed-eligibility intake route without required clinical review. A human-like organization that insists every case visit every review agent can therefore impose the wrong sequence. Conversely, a limited delegated nonclinical approval is not permission for an administrative worker to make unrestricted medical-necessity denials. S09 and prior D11/D12 provide scoped context; the current case/tool contract governs execution.

**Notice invariant:** its reason/outcome/recipient/version derives from the actual authorized case state. A notice capability does not decide the outcome by improving the prose. A successful external reversal remains distinct from its effectuation and downstream care/payment.

**Three organizational options:**

| Option | Benefit to investigate | Main failure to challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One owner with administrative/review modes | Low communication overhead and shared evidence context | Review-stage or permission confusion |
| Owner with focused policy/clinical proposal contexts | Separate attention and potentially better contradiction detection | Correlated errors, excess objections or stale proposals |
| Task-relevant intake/review/notice workers | Persistent local objectives and explicit handoffs | Unnecessary stage activation, conflicting writes, invented independence |

**Provisional conclusion:** merge some case operations, preserve review-stage/authority distinctions, and compare context separation rather than assume it helps. If a benchmark task assigns the agent a simulated professional review, it may perform that function through the allowed tools; the design must not invent an unrelated real-world person requirement. A software critic still cannot claim to be an actual independent physician or external adjudicator. Relevant decisions: AD05/AD06/AD10/AD14; T03–T08/T14/T19.

## JD03 — Care-management conversation and coordination owner

**Functions considered together:** M01 care coordination, M09/M10/M11 navigation/support, M13 general education support, M18 transition coordination and M30 administrative follow-up. Specialized psychosocial, medication, rehabilitation/nutrition and receiving-service work remains explicitly scoped.

**Question:** does one maintained dialogue/case owner better preserve patient responses and open service obligations, or does it become overloaded and need a dedicated conversation worker?

**Inputs:** actual instruction and permitted chart, prior outreach state, questions already asked, actual answers/refusals, goals, representation facts, assessments required by the task, available service responses and unresolved barriers. Keep patient statements, chart facts and model interpretations distinct.

**Proposed responsibilities:** choose the next relevant question from actual gaps; preserve answers and speaker identity; maintain goals and unresolved needs; coordinate the appropriate plan/referrals; observe acceptance and follow-through; record the permitted terminal disposition. Do not infer consent, comprehension, resource capacity or delivery from optimistic planning.

**Continuity contract:** a context refresh carries case identity, source/version references, last observed dialogue position, answers already obtained, unresolved items, patient preferences/refusal and the next accountable obligation. A message handoff is not automatically comprehension or an accepted transfer of responsibility.

**Three organizational options:**

| Option | Benefit to investigate | Main failure to challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One conversation/coordination owner | Preserves the relationship between what was said and what is planned | Context pressure, repetitive questioning or neglected follow-up |
| Owner plus bounded specialist assessments | Focuses expertise while preserving one dialogue history | Specialists infer facts the patient never supplied |
| Separate conversation and service-coordination workers | Allows sustained focus on interaction versus tracking | Lost refusals, duplicated questions or unowned obligations |

**Provisional conclusion:** continuity argues for a merged owner as one baseline, while actual dialogue/context failures could favor a dedicated conversation worker. Real-world service workers remain observed counterparties; a model transport/aide persona cannot make physical completion true. Relevant decisions: AD02/AD07/AD09/AD16; T09–T13/T17/T18/T20.

## A fair role-worker alternative

The separate-worker design should be given the same substantive knowledge and a competent communication contract. Activate only roles relevant to the current task; permit a role catalogue without instantiating every role. Give workers bounded goals, explicit evidence/permission views, responsible ownership and stop conditions. Allow clarification and appropriate ordered execution rather than forcing every role to act in parallel.

Its potential benefits include focused context, independent exploration, persistent local obligations and enforceable isolation where the actual environment supports it. Its potential costs include repeated evidence transfer, coordination latency, divergent state and extra inference. Neither set of effects has been measured here. Separate professional labels alone do not determine which effects occur.

The experimental comparison is therefore a substantive choice between organizations, not a comparison of a carefully engineered single owner with a deliberately weak collection of personas. Role richness, permission scope, shared state, worker count, autonomy and model choice should be varied transparently rather than bundled into one unexplained change.
