# Sources for the real-world healthcare role map

Research date: October 17, 2026. Supports [WORKFLOW_PEOPLE.md](../WORKFLOW_PEOPLE.md). Public sources only; no protected handbook content was used.

## Method and limits

Searched government guidance, providers' own department/job descriptions, health plans' policies, professional associations and the official benchmark. Relevant passages were inspected; not every page of each long PDF was read. The role map synthesizes these sources rather than treating one organization's staffing as universal. Job descriptions establish concrete responsibilities, not mandatory staffing or continued vacancy availability. Older examples do not establish current headcounts.

Below, opened text and indexed excerpts are distinguished. Summaries are paraphrases. PDF page numbers are one-based.

## Benchmark

- **R01 — [Official benchmark repository](https://github.com/actava-ai/chi-bench#what-this-benchmark-measures).** Read live in this conversation. Defines provider authorization, payer UM and care management; does not establish every real-world role below.
- **R02 — [Public task explorer](https://www.actava.ai/benchmarks/tasks).** Read public overview/examples. Tasks have defined starting points; not every real-world participant is individually simulated.

## Provider operations

- **R03 — [Mayo: Precertification and Authorization Representative](https://jobs.mayoclinic.org/job/rochester/precertification-and-authorization-rep-hybrid/33647/100997191200).** Opened responsibilities/qualifications; posting September 22, 2026. Referral, authorization, pre-appointment insurance review, patient access and denial recovery.
- **R04 — [UR Medicine Noyes job descriptions](https://www.urmc.rochester.edu/noyes/careers-noyes/current-job-openings).** Opened relevant sections. Authorization specialist follows requests through appeals; separate scheduling, patient-relations, social-work and pharmacy roles.
- **R05 — [Michigan: Patient Services Intermediate](https://careernavigator.umich.edu/job_detail/103767/patient-services-intermediate).** Indexed description inspected; open/find did not expose matching body. Scheduling, insurance approval, authorization coordination and code entry can coexist in one job.
- **R06 — [UCSD: billing and insurance FAQs](https://health.ucsd.edu/insurance-billing/paying/faq/).** Relevant indexed body inspected. Benefits review and financial counselors; authorization and patient financial responsibility are distinct.
- **R07 — [AHIMA: evolving ROI training](https://journal.ahima.org/page/ensure-roi-training-evolves-as-the-industry-changes).** Opened relevant text. Release-of-information specialists process record requests and safeguard appropriate access/privacy.
- **R08 — [AHIMA: denials-management toolkit announcement](https://journal.ahima.org/AMP_EDN/383/AHIMA-Releases-Denials-Management-Toolkit-1776.amp.html).** Indexed article body inspected; March 16, 2018. CDI/coding and denials roles; principally adjacent claims work, not every pre-service request.
- **R09 — [ASHP: specialty-pharmacy technicians](https://news.ashp.org/news/ashp-news/2019/11/21/Specialty-Pharmacy-Technicians-Assume-Advanced-Roles).** Opened relevant passages. UC Davis/UNC examples of authorization paperwork, payer follow-up, appeals logistics and assistance access alongside clinicians. Historical examples, not current staffing counts.
- **R10 — [AMCP: managed-care pharmacists](https://www.amcp.org/about/managed-care-pharmacy/what-do-managed-care-pharmacists-do).** Relevant indexed body inspected. Medication review, PA programs, evidence evaluation, medication management and pharmacy-benefit work.
- **R11 — [CMS: power mobility devices](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/fee-schedules/durable-medical-equipment-prosthetic-devices-prosthetics-orthotics-supplies/power-mobility-devices).** Relevant indexed instructions inspected. Ordering practitioner/supplier authorization and fulfillment; specific equipment pathway.

## Payer review and appeals

- **R12 — [Martin's Point: medical necessity criteria](https://martinspoint.org/-/media/Providers/Documents/Clinical-Documentation/Medical-Necessity-Criteria-Standard.ashx).** Opened five-page policy, modified April 20, 2026; page 3 relevant. Nonclinical information collection, licensed clinical review, physician-adviser escalation and committee oversight. Local example, not universal staffing law.
- **R13 — [Arizona Priority Care: 2026 UM program](https://azprioritycare.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026-AZPC-Utilization-Management-Program.pdf).** Opened relevant portions of 21 pages, especially pages 5–6. Intake, eligibility, routing, notices, clinical review, physician/behavioral oversight. Do not generalize local decision authority nationally.
- **R14 — [UCSD: RN Manager, Utilization Management](https://employment.ucsd.edu/rn-manager-utilization-management-140306/job/2BD7908FE0FC2DE4369E0F492FCDD518).** Opened responsibilities; posted June 26, 2026. Nurse case managers/referral coordinators, supervision, escalation, delegated UM, reporting and regulatory work.
- **R15 — [UHC: appeals and reconsiderations](https://www.uhcprovider.com/en/claims-payments-billing/appeals.html).** Opened relevant text. Provider/medical-director discussion; representation and consent distinctions; process varies by plan/state. Peer discussion and formal appeal are not interchangeable.
- **R16 — [CMS: organization determinations](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/appeals-grievances/managed-care/organization-determinations).** Public body inspected. Enrollee, representative and provider request roles in Medicare health-plan context.
- **R17 — [CMS: federal external review](https://www.cms.gov/cciio/programs-and-initiatives/consumer-support-and-information/csg-ext-appeals-facts).** Opened relevant text. Independent reviewers and administrative contractor; applicable-plan scope, not every plan's appeal route.
- **R18 — [Cigna: healthcare coverage policies](https://www.cigna.com/legal/members/member-rights-and-responsibilities/cigna-health-care-policies).** Relevant public text inspected. Medical directors, independent experts, technology assessment and physician committees; case review versus governance.

## Patient and communication

- **R19 — [HHS: personal representatives](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/personal-representatives/index.html).** Opened relevant text. Family support and formal healthcare authority differ; scope depends on applicable circumstances/law.
- **R20 — [HHS OMHA: right to representation](https://www.hhs.gov/about/agencies/omha/filing-an-appeal/your-right-to-representation/index.html).** Public text inspected. Appointment/scope in Medicare appeals; not automatically equivalent to treatment-decision authority.
- **R21 — [HHS: telehealth communication access](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/disability/guidance-on-nondiscrimination-in-telehealth/index.html).** Relevant passages inspected. Interpreter/translator and communication-access roles; used for role identification, not a statement of every current legal requirement.

## Care management

- **R22 — [AHRQ: care coordination](https://www.ahrq.gov/ncepcr/care/coordination.html).** Opened overview. Assessment, shared plans, transitions, follow-up and community-resource coordination.
- **R23 — [AHRQ-hosted care-management implementation guide](https://www.ahrq.gov/sites/default/files/wysiwyg/evidencenow/tools-and-materials/care-management-implementation-guide.pdf).** Opened 111-page document; inspected relevant indexed role discussion, especially printed page 15. Nurses/social workers common; pharmacists, behavioral professionals, dietitians and educators also possible. Older guide, not a licensing standard.
- **R24 — [AHRQ PSNet: GRACE model](https://psnet.ahrq.gov/innovation/team-developed-care-plan-and-ongoing-care-management-social-workers-and-nurse).** Opened relevant portions. NP/social-worker pair, physician, pharmacy, behavioral-health and patient/family participation. One model, not a required team everywhere.
- **R25 — [UCLA: clinical case managers](https://www.uclahealth.org/programs/ccsw/about-us/rn-clinical-case-managers).** Relevant body inspected. Nursing, social work, advanced practice, transitions and psychosocial support. Qualifications are organization-specific.
- **R26 — [UCLA: care-coordination department](https://www.uclahealth.org/programs/ccsw/about-us).** Relevant body inspected. Administrative staff, case managers, social workers and NPs across hospital/transitions.
- **R27 — [UCLA: hospital staff roles](https://www.uclahealth.org/hospitals/santa-monica/patients-visitors/know-your-hospital-staff).** Relevant body inspected. Case managers, discharge planners, social workers, dietitians, rehabilitation, pharmacy and spiritual care.
- **R28 — [CDC: patient navigation](https://www.cdc.gov/cancer/php/interventions/patient-navigation.html).** Relevant text inspected. Access barriers, navigation and possible nurse/social-worker/CHW backgrounds; cancer-program context.
- **R29 — [CDC: community health workers](https://www.cdc.gov/chronic-disease/php/community-health-worker-resources/index.html).** Relevant text inspected. Trusted community role connecting people with health/social services.
- **R30 — [Medicare: home health services](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/home-health-services).** Relevant body inspected. Nursing, PT/OT/speech-language, social services, aides, provider orders and agency coordination. Role examples, not payment/eligibility advice.
- **R31 — [Mayo: palliative care](https://www.mayoclinic.org/departments-centers/general-internal-medicine/palliative-care-minnesota/overview?p=1).** Relevant body inspected; page April 22, 2025. Physician, advanced-practice, nursing, pharmacy, nutrition, social-work and chaplain team; conditional serious-illness example.
- **R32 — [Partnership HealthPlan: 2026 care-coordination program](https://www.partnershiphp.org/Providers/HealthServices/Documents/Physicians_Advisory_Committee_PAC/2026/04-April/Policy_Summary/Care_Coordination/MPCD2013_clean.pdf).** Opened relevant portions, especially pages 12–17. Case managers, social workers, guides, supervisors, trainers, analysts, clinical/policy advisers and administrative support. Future expansion text referencing January 2027 was not treated as already operational.
- **R33 — [UCLA: respiratory care](https://www.uclahealth.org/medical-services/respiratory-care).** Relevant indexed text inspected. Respiratory therapists provide cardiopulmonary care as part of a wider team; conditional clinical-service partner.
- **R34 — [Medicare: hospice benefits](https://www.medicare.gov/publications/02154-medicare-hospice-benefits.pdf).** Relevant indexed text inspected. Hospice team/attending professional and family support; specific end-of-life pathway, separate from general palliative care.
- **R35 — [Medicare: help with rights and protections](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/your-medicare-rights/get-help-with-your-rights-protections).** Relevant indexed text inspected. SHIP counseling and appropriate complaint/help contacts; Medicare-specific consumer assistance, not ordinary clinical review.

## Exclusions and access limits

Some NCQA FAQs were indexed but a direct request returned HTTP 403; no binding current staffing requirement was inferred. NCQA proposed-standard PDFs were drafts/obsolete consultation material and were excluded from final-requirement claims. Aetna overview fetch failed; old Aetna/UHC manuals were not needed for the central map. An old AHIMA ROI toolkit link redirected to not-found; accessible article R07 replaced it.

Reddit, Wikipedia, recruiter reposts and vendor marketing were not used to establish clinical authority. No interviews, observed staffing census, accreditation audit or clinical validation was performed.
