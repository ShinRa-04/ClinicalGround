## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Route a challenge through the correct dispute process.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONTEXTUAL: The appeal process requires proper case handling and representation, but a specific national appeal-coordinator job is not mandated by these sources. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Establish appeal versus grievance category.
- Check representation and record completeness.
- Route independent review and track communications.
