## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL: Practical nursing tasks, reporting and state-dependent permissions are explicit in the occupational description and scope framework. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.
