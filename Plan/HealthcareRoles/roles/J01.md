## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Decide the appeal within the authorized hearing/adjudication jurisdiction.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: The MA appeal overview and effectuation rule recognize eligible adjudication and attorney-adjudicator dispositions. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review properly presented issues and record.
- Apply governing process.
- Issue decision/disposition.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Affirm/reverse/remand as permitted by the actual appeal process.
