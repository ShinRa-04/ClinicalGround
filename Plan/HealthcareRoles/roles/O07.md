# O07 — Medical coding specialist

Domain: **provider**. Research date: 2026-10-17. [Role index](../ROLE_INDEX.md) · [Decision framework](../AUTHORITY.md)

## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Translate documented services and diagnoses into appropriate codes.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_GENERAL_DESCRIPTION: Coding supported clinical information is directly documented; queries and clinician clarification are supported by the documentation example. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review documentation.
- Assign/reconcile codes using applicable guidance.
- Query unclear support through approved channels.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Select supported codes and flag a coding inconsistency.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Cannot create a clinical diagnosis or change the underlying facts.
- Coding does not decide treatment or authorize coverage.

## Incoming information and service context

**Status:** SYNTHESIZED MODEL: suggested information needs, NOT CONFIRMED institutional requirements.

- Authenticated documentation.
- Relevant coding requirements.

## Outputs and responsibility for the work
