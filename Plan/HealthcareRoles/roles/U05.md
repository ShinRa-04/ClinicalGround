## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Provide specialist expertise to the designated reviewing decision-maker.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_ORGANIZATION_EXAMPLE: Insurer public material describes specialist expertise helping medical directors with complex cases. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Assess a defined specialty question.
- Evaluate relevant evidence.
- State rationale and limitations.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Give a specialty opinion.
- Decide a case only if separately appointed and authorized.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Consultation alone is not final appeal authority.
- Independence must not be assumed from the word consultant.
