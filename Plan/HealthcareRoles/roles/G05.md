## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: A supervisor title does not imply identical clinical powers or a universal ordering between professional licenses.

R32 distinguishes RN, LVN, nonclinical and social-work supervisors; those qualifications must not be collapsed into one scope.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
