## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Coordinate receipt of the patient and information at the receiving facility.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** COMPOSITE_FUNCTION_WITH_SOURCE_CONTEXT: Sources establish transfer to receiving facilities and information exchange, not one nationally standardized receiving-coordinator occupation. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Check service capability and transfer information.
- Arrange handoff and assigned clinical assessment.
- Report acceptance or unresolved needs.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Operational acceptance within facility authority.
- Route clinical decisions to responsible practitioners.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title
