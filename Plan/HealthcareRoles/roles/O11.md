## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Present or assess the provider's clinical position in a review dispute.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_PROCESS_EXAMPLE: Payer documentation supports clinician peer discussion and clarifies its relationship to pre-service appeal. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review facts and disputed criteria.
- Discuss clinical support with reviewer.
- Document discussion and communicate next steps.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Give a clinical opinion and recommend revision/escalation.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Peer discussion does not itself confer payer decision authority.
- Cannot assume it substitutes for a formal appeal.
