## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Apply the plan's administrative membership and benefit information.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_ORGANIZATION_EXAMPLE: Administrative benefit evaluation and assigned benefit-only decisions are explicit in the program. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Check enrollment and relevant benefits.
- Reconcile administrative discrepancies.
- Route questions needing interpretation.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Make administrative benefit determinations only within delegated authority.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Administrative benefit issue is not interchangeable with adverse medical necessity.
- Cannot create clinical rationale for a denial.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: A benefits title alone does not establish signatory power. Administrative coverage exclusions and clinical medical necessity need different reasoning/review.

Exact benefit authority and decision notice are plan-specific; not every benefits specialist is authorized to issue a denial. R13 permits some assigned benefit-only decisions; actual signatory authority still requires the plan/delegation.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
