## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Coordinate acceptance and services for a home-health plan.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: Home-health acceptance, coordinated disciplines and physician/allowed-practitioner plan/order responsibilities are described. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Assess whether agency can meet needs.
- Coordinate ordered disciplines.
- Communicate changes and plan revisions.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Accept/coordinate services within agency authority.
- Seek authorized plan changes.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Not unrestricted authority to rewrite prescriber orders.
- Acceptance is not proof every ordered visit occurred.
