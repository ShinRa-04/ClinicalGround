## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Provide medical specialty care for psychiatric needs.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_GENERAL_DESCRIPTION: Psychiatry is documented as a physician specialty; the card restricts medical treatment to actual privileges and patient context. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Assess psychiatric condition and relevant medical history.
- Recommend treatment.
- Manage authorized medication care and coordinate follow-up.
