## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Resolve assigned health-information access and privacy questions.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONTEXTUAL: The program identifies privacy-officer functions; HHS describes organization access/disclosure policies and purpose-dependent requirements. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review purpose, authority and applicable requirements.
- Support appropriate disclosure/access.
- Guide staff on privacy concerns.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Privacy/access determinations within mandate and governing law.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Cannot decide treatment or benefit coverage merely by controlling record access.
- No blanket claim that all sharing needs authorization or that all treatment data are unrestricted.
