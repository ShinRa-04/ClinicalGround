## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

**Status:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: The specified MA reconsideration requires prior noninvolvement and appropriate physician expertise for medical-necessity denial. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Apply governing criteria.
