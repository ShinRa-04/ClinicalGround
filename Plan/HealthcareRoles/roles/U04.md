## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Perform assigned clinical coverage review and document the rationale.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: The MA requirement supports qualified initial review, while insurer material illustrates physician case-review functions. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review patient-specific evidence and criteria.
- Discuss unresolved clinical issues.
- Make the delegated determination and communicate rationale.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: Initial review, final institutional accountability and independent reconsideration are separate; medical director is not a universal superior over the treating clinician.

Case-review function is distinct from organization-wide CMO governance; authority depends on product, license, expertise and delegation. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
