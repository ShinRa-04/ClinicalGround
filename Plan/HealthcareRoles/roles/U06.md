## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Review behavioral-health service requests within the assigned professional scope.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL: The program documents behavioral-health oversight and service-appropriate qualified review. Oversight and individual case decision authority must be distinguished. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review relevant clinical record and criteria.
- Seek appropriate clarification.
- Document assessment and route decisions.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Make delegated review decisions compatible with professional qualifications.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Reviewer role does not establish a treating relationship.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: A designated behavioral program leader is not automatically the reviewer of every case; counselor, psychologist and physician qualifications are not interchangeable.

Psychiatrist, psychologist and counselor credentials are not interchangeable; apply actual product and review requirements. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
