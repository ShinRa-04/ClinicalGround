## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Clarify administrative coverage information needed to arrange a service.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_ORGANIZATION_EXAMPLE: Provider sources directly distinguish eligibility/benefits and patient responsibility from authorization/payment. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Check eligibility/benefit information.
- Identify authorization requirements.
- Communicate unresolved plan details and potential patient obligations.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Record verified benefits information and route discrepancies.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Benefits verification is not a medical-necessity approval.
- Cannot promise final payment solely from eligibility.
