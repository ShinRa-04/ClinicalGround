## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Create and route a reviewable request record for the reviewing organization.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_ORGANIZATION_EXAMPLE: The program explicitly assigns intake, data entry, information requests, routing and notification to nonclinical staff. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Capture request details.
- Check completeness and request missing information.
- Route and track case status.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Make operational decisions and only explicitly delegated limited nonclinical/benefit decisions under the applicable program criteria.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.
