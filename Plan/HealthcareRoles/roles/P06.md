## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Render written information accurately in another language.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_GENERAL_DESCRIPTION: Written translation is distinct from interpretation; fidelity of content is supported. Escalation of ambiguous wording is a proposed quality-control action. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Translate the supplied document.
- Preserve material meaning and terminology.
- Flag ambiguity in the source text.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Select faithful terminology and seek source clarification.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Cannot change the determination or add clinical advice.
- Translation does not itself prove patient understanding.
