## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Evaluate medication evidence and pharmacy-program requirements.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL: The professional description supports medication-program review; the program example permits pharmacy-specific professional denial review. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review drug-related criteria.
- Identify medication evidence needs.
- Document recommendations and authorized outcomes.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: Do not apply a medical-service Part C appeal rule to every drug benefit. Coverage review and prescribing remain different authorities.

Medication pathways can differ from medical-service prior authorization and Part C item/service appeals. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
