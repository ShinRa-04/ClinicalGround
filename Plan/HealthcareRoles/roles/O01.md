## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Move a supported request through the provider's authorization workflow.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_ORGANIZATION_EXAMPLE: Accessible provider descriptions support assembling relevant record information, tracking requests and appeals. Expired Mayo R03 is not needed for support. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Check request requirements.
- Assemble existing evidence.
- Submit and track requests.
- Coordinate responses and record outcomes.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: Requests, receipt, approval, scheduling and delivery are distinct events; title alone does not grant clinical decision authority.

Works for requester; not payer intake. Local payer requirements and request stage determine the needed packet.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
