# M01 — Lead care manager / case manager

Domain: **care**. Research date: 2026-10-17. [Role index](../ROLE_INDEX.md) · [Decision framework](../AUTHORITY.md)

**Evidence warning:** REVIEWED within the frozen assertion inventory: scoped evidence judgments, alternatives and unresolved facts recorded; not universal certification. Actual local authority: **NOT CONFIRMED**. See [evidence register](../EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md).

## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Coordinate a patient-centered plan and track unresolved needs.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_MULTIPLE_PROGRAM_EXAMPLES: Several accessible programs demonstrate assessment, shared planning, coordination and follow-through, with different staffing. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Assess goals and barriers.
- Coordinate services and responsibilities.
- Monitor follow-through and revise coordination work.
- Communicate changes.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Prioritize coordination tasks and propose plan changes.
- Make professional decisions only within underlying qualifications.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Title alone is not prescribing authority.
- A written referral is not proof a service was received.

## Incoming information and service context

**Status:** SYNTHESIZED MODEL: suggested information needs, NOT CONFIRMED institutional requirements.

- Patient preferences and assessment.
- Clinical plan and service responses.

## Outputs and responsibility for the work

**Status:** SYNTHESIZED MODEL: suggested work products, NOT CONFIRMED mandatory records/forms.

- Shared coordination plan.
- Assigned follow-up and unresolved-need record.

The role holder is responsible for the accuracy/provenance of their own work within the assignment. This is a functional responsibility statement, not a legal allocation of all institutional liability.

**Status:** SYNTHESIZED MODEL: suggested routing, NOT CONFIRMED local escalation procedure.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: These examples do not establish national prevalence. Coordination title is not a license or independent prescribing credential; unavailable R23 is not needed to establish these examples.

Credential may be nursing, social work or another background; separate coordination authority from that credential. Published staffing examples differ; this is not evidence of national role prevalence.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
