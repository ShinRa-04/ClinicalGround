## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Coordinate a feasible transition and transfer necessary information.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: The cited hospital rule directly supports needs/access evaluation, patient discussion, qualified planning and transfer information. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Assess post-discharge needs/access.
- Coordinate available receiving services.
- Discuss plan with patient/supports.
- Transfer relevant information and pending issues.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: Medical readiness, discharge planning, receiving acceptance, coverage and patient rights are distinct; planner title is not sole authority.

Hospital 482.43 permits development by or under RN, social worker or other qualified personnel; do not impose RN-only staffing nationally.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
