## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Provide advanced nursing/clinical care within authorized practice.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL: Advanced practice duties are described generally; the California regulator gives concrete alternative practice categories and employer conditions. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Assess the patient.
- Develop or contribute to clinical plans.
- Order and evaluate services where authorized.
- Counsel and coordinate follow-up.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Diagnose, order or prescribe only where authorized by applicable practice rules and privileges.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- NP title does not grant insurance appeal or policy authority.
