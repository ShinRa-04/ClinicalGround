Domain: **care**. Research date: 2026-10-17. [Role index](../ROLE_INDEX.md) · [Decision framework](../AUTHORITY.md)

## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Assess nursing needs and provide/co-ordinate nursing care.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_GENERAL_DESCRIPTION: RN assessment, treatment, teaching, observation and communication are supported as general duties; task permission is constrained separately. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Assess and observe.
- Administer treatments as authorized.
- Document response.
- Teach patient/caregiver and communicate changes.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Make nursing judgments and carry out authorized care.
- Escalate unexpected findings.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- RN status alone is not prescribing or payer-decision authority.
- Delegation does not transfer all accountability to unlicensed staff.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: Assignment is not evidence of legal delegation; additional APRN authority cannot be inferred from RN status alone.

A direct-care RN, payer UM nurse and nurse case manager are different functions even if one person can hold more than one role.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
