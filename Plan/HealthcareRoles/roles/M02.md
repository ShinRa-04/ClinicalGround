## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Assess psychosocial and practical support needs and connect appropriate resources.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL: Social assessment, resources, coping and coordination are supported. The prior label nonclinical would be too restrictive for all medical social workers. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Assess social situation/supports.
- Help coping and access.
- Advocate/referral and follow up.
- Document relevant findings.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Choose appropriate social-service referrals within role.
- Identify need for licensed clinical or protection support.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Do not infer authority to diagnose or provide psychotherapy without required clinical qualification.
- Cannot decide insurance coverage.

## Incoming information and service context

**Status:** SYNTHESIZED MODEL: suggested information needs, NOT CONFIRMED institutional requirements.

- Patient goals and social assessment.
- Available resources.

## Outputs and responsibility for the work

The role holder is responsible for the accuracy/provenance of their own work within the assignment. This is a functional responsibility statement, not a legal allocation of all institutional liability.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: UCLA describes an unlicensed Clinical Social Worker II role. A job title containing clinical does not establish independent clinical licensure.

Job titles and licensure differ; distinguish supportive casework from licensed clinical treatment. UCLA describes a pre-licensure Clinical Social Worker II assignment; clinical job wording does not establish independent clinical licensure.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
