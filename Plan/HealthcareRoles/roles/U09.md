## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Deliver the authorized outcome with accurate procedural information.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_ORGANIZATION_EXAMPLE: Program duties and notice content are explicit. A notification task is not identical to making the substantive clinical decision. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Use the recorded determination.
- Prepare/distribute required notices.
- Track communication failures and corrections.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Apply notice/communication workflow.
- Escalate mismatch or missing rationale.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Notice sent is not evidence that services were delivered.
