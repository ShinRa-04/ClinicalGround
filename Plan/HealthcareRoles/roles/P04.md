## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Pursue a coverage dispute within the appointment and applicable appeal route.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: Appointment and scope requirements are directly documented in the Medicare representation guidance; provider benefits assignment is distinct. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Establish representation.
- Gather and transmit the appeal record.
- Track communications and explain next procedural steps.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.
