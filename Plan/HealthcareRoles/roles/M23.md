## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Arrange the practical transport needed for an agreed journey.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONTEXT_WITH_MODELED_DUTIES: Medicaid guidance supports several transport-administration models. The detailed dispatch workflow remains a proposed service interface. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Confirm trip details and suitable service.
- Arrange provider/driver.
- Communicate booking changes or failure.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Operational booking/routing within service rules.

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.
