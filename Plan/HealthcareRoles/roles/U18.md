## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

**Status:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: The relevant external-review guidance supports an independent outcome binding on the insurer within its covered process. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.
