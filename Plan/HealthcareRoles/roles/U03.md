## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: R13 contains both medical-director final responsibility and a wider professional-denial list; exact signing/review authority requires its specific policy/delegation and product.

Under MA 422.566(d), anticipated adverse medical necessity requires appropriate licensed professional review; local policy may impose stricter physician escalation. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
