Domain: **care**. Research date: 2026-10-17. [Role index](../ROLE_INDEX.md) · [Decision framework](../AUTHORITY.md)

## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Support safe and effective medication use.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL: Medication review, counseling and professional communication are general duties. Washington provides a concrete delegated drug-therapy pathway. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review prescription and medication risks.
- Counsel on use.
- Resolve medication-related questions with prescribers.
- Supervise assigned technician work.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Make pharmacy professional judgments.
- Modify/initiate therapy only when separately authorized.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Cannot assume unrestricted prescribing.
- A clinical recommendation does not itself change payer coverage.

## Incoming information and service context

**Status:** SYNTHESIZED MODEL: suggested information needs, NOT CONFIRMED institutional requirements.

- Prescription and medication history.
- Relevant clinical information.

## Outputs and responsibility for the work

**Status:** SYNTHESIZED MODEL: suggested work products, NOT CONFIRMED mandatory records/forms.

- Verified dispensing/counseling record.
- Medication recommendation or clarification request.

The role holder is responsible for the accuracy/provenance of their own work within the assignment. This is a functional responsibility statement, not a legal allocation of all institutional liability.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: Do not imply every change requires a fresh individual physician instruction or every pharmacist can independently prescribe; actual legal/program authority controls.

Professional scope, collaborative arrangements and specific state permissions must be identified for therapy changes. Washington CDTA is one documented pathway for specified therapy activity; no universal requirement for a new physician instruction on each authorized action is inferred.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
