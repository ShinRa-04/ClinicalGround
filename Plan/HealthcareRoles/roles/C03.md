## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Provide condition- or procedure-specific clinical evaluation and recommendations.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_GENERAL_DESCRIPTION: Specialty clinical assessment and treatment are documented, while referral/return communication is supported as coordination. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Review the referral and relevant evidence.
- Assess the specialty problem.
- Document proposed service and communicate findings.

## Decisions potentially within scope

**Status:** CONDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: actual license, delegation and local decision permission are NOT CONFIRMED.

- Make specialty recommendations/orders within privileges.
- Decide clinical readiness within assigned responsibility.

## Authority not conferred merely by the title

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY: not an exhaustive legal prohibition; another qualification or appointment can change scope.

- Specialty expertise is not unilateral coverage authority.
- Does not erase the patient's participation or other clinicians' responsibilities.

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: Specialty expertise does not itself establish final signoff over every PCP decision or coverage.

Clinical responsibility must be assigned for the actual service; specialist is not necessarily the organizational supervisor of the PCP.
