## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Coordinate hospice care while distinguishing interdisciplinary, RN coordination, attending and certifying/admission functions.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_DATED_CODIFICATION_WITH_DISTINCT_AUTHORITIES: Official 2025 codification provides stronger evidence than the earlier 2024 snippet: IDG, designated RN coordination, physician certification and admission functions are distinct. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Prepare and review an interdisciplinary written care plan as specified in the cited hospice edition.
- Use the designated interdisciplinary-group RN coordination function described in that edition.
- Keep attending, terminal-illness certification and admission recommendation responsibilities distinct.

## Scope, reporting and authority conditions

**Status:** SCOPED CONDITIONS / UNRESOLVED LOCAL FACTS: The IDG is a group, not one individual. Evidence is expressly the October 2025 codification; complete current 2026 hospice-rule verification is not claimed.

V11, V16 and V17 are the official October 2025 codification, not a complete current-2026 update check. They distinguish IDG/designated RN coordination, physician certification and admission. A patient-designated attending may have a different allowed credential; that does not grant every attending terminal-illness certification authority.

A reporting supervisor is not invented from this title. See the explicitly scoped [reporting examples](../graphs/reporting-examples.svg). Reporting rank does not itself decide consent, clinical scope, coverage or appeal authority.
