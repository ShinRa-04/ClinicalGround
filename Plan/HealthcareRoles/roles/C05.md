## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Provide clinical assessment and treatment within the applicable PA practice arrangement.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL: Clinical PA duties have general support. Oregon demonstrates a collaboration framework and associate terminology, preventing a universal supervision inference. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Examine and assess.
- Order/interpret tests where authorized.
- Treat and educate.
- Record progress and coordinate with the clinical team.
