## Purpose

**Status:** INTERPRETATION: functional summary, not an independently verified occupational classification.

Review the applicable appealed adjudicative decision under Council jurisdiction.

## Responsibilities

**Status:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: Council review is a named MA level and the effectuation rule contains a Council-review exception. Individual exceptions are in the assertion audit.

- Consider eligible request and record.
- Apply review procedure.
- Issue the authorized disposition.
