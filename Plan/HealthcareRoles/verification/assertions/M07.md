## ROLE:M07:mission:01

**Original:** Provide qualified psychological assessment and treatment.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Psychological assessment/treatment is supported; New Mexico documents additional prescribing-certificate pathways.

**Alternatives:** General psychology license versus additional conditional/full prescribing certification in a jurisdiction that provides it.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Neither a universal prescribing right nor a blanket nationwide prohibition is justified; specific current qualification must be checked.
