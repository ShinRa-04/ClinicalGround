## ROLE:G13:mission:01

**Original:** Support reliable health-information systems and authorized workflows.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Health-information system implementation, validation, education and integrity support are described.

**Alternatives:** Clinical informaticist with an additional license versus technical/application professional without that clinical qualification.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Technical configuration permission is not permission to alter clinical facts or approve care.
