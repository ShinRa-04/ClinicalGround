## ROLE:U03:mission:01

**Original:** Assess the submitted clinical evidence under the assigned review program.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Clinical review/approval and escalation are directly described in example policies; profession alone does not establish authority for a particular product.

**Alternatives:** First-level approval, clarification or escalation; some administrative decisions can be delegated without the same clinical review route.

**Complexity / unresolved:** R13 contains both medical-director final responsibility and a wider professional-denial list; exact signing/review authority requires its specific policy/delegation and product.

## ROLE:U03:condition:01

**Original:** Under MA 422.566(d), anticipated adverse medical necessity requires appropriate licensed professional review; local policy may impose stricter physician escalation.

**Verdict:** SCOPED_CONDITION_OR_EXPLICIT_UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_FACT

**Reason:** Conditions are limitations on use, not assertions that an unidentified local case satisfies them. R13 contains both medical-director final responsibility and a wider professional-denial list; exact signing/review authority requires its specific policy/delegation and product.

**Corrected wording:** Under MA 422.566(d), anticipated adverse medical necessity requires appropriate licensed professional review; local policy may impose stricter physician escalation. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.

**Alternatives:** First-level approval, clarification or escalation; some administrative decisions can be delegated without the same clinical review route.
