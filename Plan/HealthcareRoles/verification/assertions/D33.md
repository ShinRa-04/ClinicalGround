# D33 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D33:rule

**Original:** CMS 2024 guidance describes qualified interdisciplinary roles; members retain distinct professional scope.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_DATED_RULES_WITH_ADDED_DISTINCTIONS

**Reason:** Official October 2025 codification strengthens the earlier limited composition snapshot and separates IDG coordination, attending, certification and admission.

**Corrected wording:** The official October 2025 hospice codification distinguishes the interdisciplinary group/designated RN coordination from attending, physician certification and admission responsibilities.

**Alternatives:** Designated RN coordination; specified physicians certify/admit within the codified rules; attending may be a different allowed professional.

**Complexity / unresolved:** These are distinct decisions and a group is not one professional. A complete current-2026 regulatory update audit is not claimed from the dated codification.
