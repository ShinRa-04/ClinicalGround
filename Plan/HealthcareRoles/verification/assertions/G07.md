## ROLE:G07:mission:01

**Original:** Develop and deliver the assigned workforce training.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Training-program design, materials and implementation are described.

**Alternatives:** Clinical educator, nonclinical trainer or program-specific training specialist.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Course attendance does not grant a license or establish competence for every clinical task.
