## ROLE:U12:mission:01

**Original:** Route a challenge through the correct dispute process.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The appeal process requires proper case handling and representation, but a specific national appeal-coordinator job is not mandated by these sources.

**Alternatives:** Appeals and grievance operations may be combined or separated; a delegated initial reviewer may forward appeals to the plan.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Routing/standing checks are operational responsibilities; clinical reconsideration authority must be separately qualified.
