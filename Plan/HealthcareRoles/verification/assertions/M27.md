# M27 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:M27:mission:01

**Original:** Contribute qualified serious-illness symptom and goals support.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The provider describes a serious-illness multidisciplinary team rather than one interchangeable profession.

**Alternatives:** Physicians, advanced-practice clinicians, nurses, pharmacy, dietetics, social work and spiritual care may contribute.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Palliative care and hospice are distinct service contexts; team membership does not give every member physician authority.
