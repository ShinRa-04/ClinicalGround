## ROLE:O02:mission:01

**Original:** Connect the referring and receiving services and close referral communication.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Provider role description directly combines referrals with scheduling/authorization, and AHRQ explains information-loss risks across referrals.

**Alternatives:** Combined role or separately staffed referral function.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Closed-loop follow-up is a coordination recommendation, not a nationally prescribed form or signoff sequence.
