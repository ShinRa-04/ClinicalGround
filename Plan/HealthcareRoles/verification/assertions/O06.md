## ROLE:O06:mission:01

**Original:** Retrieve and appropriately disclose authentic health information.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Record retrieval/disclosure roles are supported; HHS directly documents purpose-dependent minimum-necessary exceptions.

**Alternatives:** Patient access, treatment, payment and other requests can have different permission rules.

**Complexity / unresolved:** No blanket written-consent rule for every treatment disclosure; exceptions do not remove other privacy protections.
