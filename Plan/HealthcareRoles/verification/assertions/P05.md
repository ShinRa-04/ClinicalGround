## ROLE:P05:mission:01

**Original:** Enable accurate spoken or signed communication.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Government descriptions distinguish accurate interpretation and access assistance from substantive clinical decision-making.

**Alternatives:** Spoken-language or signed interpretation; other accessible communication support where appropriate.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Qualification, language and communication needs matter; no independent medical decision is conferred by interpreting.
