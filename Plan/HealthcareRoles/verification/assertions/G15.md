## ROLE:G15:mission:01

**Original:** Process payment/claim work and explain or correct administrative account issues.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Billing/account work and claim-denial support have primary occupational and medical-service context; no single universal combined job is established.

**Alternatives:** Patient accounts, insurance-claims processing, coding and provider appeal staff may be separate.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Pre-service authorization, claim adjudication, financial assistance and patient liability are different decisions.
