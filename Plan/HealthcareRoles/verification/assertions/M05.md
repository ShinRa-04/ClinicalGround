## ROLE:M05:mission:01

**Original:** Support medication operations and access logistics.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Government material supports technical/admin tasks and routing clinical questions; earlier archived ASHP material describes advanced access/appeal logistics.

**Alternatives:** Dispensing support, specialty access, refill or assistance assignments; training and state permissions differ.

**Complexity / unresolved:** An access assignment does not replace pharmacist/prescriber judgment. R09 historical examples are not proof of current staffing or procedure.
