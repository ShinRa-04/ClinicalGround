## ROLE:U08:mission:01

**Original:** Execute contracted review functions for the accountable organization.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Delegation is documented but bounded: AZPC explicitly says it is not delegated to process, determine or notify appeals in the retrieved program.

**Alternatives:** A vendor/group may handle initial UM while the health plan retains appeals, or have a different expressly documented delegation.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The same organization cannot be assumed to own the whole workflow; professional license, contractual scope and product requirements all matter.
