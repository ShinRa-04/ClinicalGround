# M06 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:M06:mission:01

**Original:** Provide medical specialty care for psychiatric needs.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Psychiatry is documented as a physician specialty; the card restricts medical treatment to actual privileges and patient context.

**Alternatives:** Treating psychiatrist, consulting psychiatrist or separately assigned reviewer are different functions.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Physician status does not automatically confer a patient relationship or payer-review delegation.
