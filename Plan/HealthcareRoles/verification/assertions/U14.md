## ROLE:U14:mission:01

**Original:** Review remaining disputed issues through the CMS-contracted independent route.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. MA remaining-dispute review and applicable effectuation duties are explicit.

**Alternatives:** Decision, remand/dismissal and eligibility for later appeal are distinct outcomes.

**Complexity / unresolved:** An IRE reviewer is not an internal supervisor; an external-review label alone does not identify the correct statutory route.
