## ROLE:M10:mission:01

**Original:** Help a person traverse appointments, referrals and practical access barriers.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. CDC describes navigation functions and different possible professional backgrounds in its cancer-program context.

**Alternatives:** Nurse, social worker, CHW or another appropriately trained navigation role.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Navigation is a function rather than one universal credential; cancer guidance does not prescribe every other program's workflow.
