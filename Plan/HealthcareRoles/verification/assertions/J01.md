## ROLE:J01:mission:01

**Original:** Decide the appeal within the authorized hearing/adjudication jurisdiction.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The MA appeal overview and effectuation rule recognize eligible adjudication and attorney-adjudicator dispositions.

**Alternatives:** Hearing/adjudication, favorable disposition, remand or other permitted outcome under the actual process.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Jurisdiction, filing and threshold conditions apply; a later stage is not automatically available or a clinical supervisory role.
