## ROLE:C03:mission:01

**Original:** Provide condition- or procedure-specific clinical evaluation and recommendations.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Specialty clinical assessment and treatment are documented, while referral/return communication is supported as coordination.

**Alternatives:** One specialist may order and perform a service; several specialists can contribute with separate responsibilities.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Specialty expertise does not itself establish final signoff over every PCP decision or coverage.

## ROLE:C03:inputs:01

**Original:** Referral question

**Verdict:** MODEL_NOT_CONFIRMED_EMPIRICAL_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_FACT

**Reason:** This is an explicit proposed field, contact, service boundary, condition or workflow-control choice. Sources inform its context but do not establish the exact design as actual or mandatory practice.

**Alternatives:** Local forms, contacts, staffing combinations and procedures may differ; the model does not select an institution-specific one.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Actual institution procedure, delegation, acknowledgement and closure rules are not supplied and remain unconfirmed.
