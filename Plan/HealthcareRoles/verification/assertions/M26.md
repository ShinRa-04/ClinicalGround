# M26 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:M26:mission:01

**Original:** Arrange a service-specific handoff and readiness check.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. General transfer/referral principles support the context; a specific infusion coordinator job, credential or national procedure is not established.

**Alternatives:** Administrative intake, nursing coordination, pharmacy and service-specific clinical readiness can be handled by different people.

**Complexity / unresolved:** This profile must remain an operational model; booking does not establish clinical readiness or administration.
