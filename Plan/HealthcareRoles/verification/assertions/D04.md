## DECISION:D04:rule

**Original:** They contribute different expertise/responsibilities; identify the clinician responsible for the disputed service.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETATION_WITH_SOURCE_CONTEXT

**Reason:** Sources describe different expertise and coordination. They do not establish a universal specialty-over-PCP command hierarchy.

**Alternatives:** Responsibility can be assigned by episode, institution, admitting/attending arrangement and privileges.

**Complexity / unresolved:** No universal hierarchy is inferred; this is an evidence boundary, not proof that no institution can assign one.
