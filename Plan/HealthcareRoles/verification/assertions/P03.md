## ROLE:P03:mission:01

**Original:** Exercise only the healthcare/information authority established for this patient.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. HHS explicitly ties representation and relevant information rights to the authority under applicable law and describes exceptions.

**Alternatives:** Broad healthcare authority or limited task/treatment authority; different rules for minors.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Authority can be limited and safety exceptions exist. No universal national family-priority order is established.
