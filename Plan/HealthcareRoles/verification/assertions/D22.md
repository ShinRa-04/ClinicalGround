# D22 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D22:rule

**Original:** 482.43(a)(5) permits development by or under an RN, social worker or other appropriately qualified personnel.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE

**Reason:** The hospital provision explicitly allows development by or under RN, social worker or other qualified personnel.

**Alternatives:** Several qualified staffing models, not RN-only planning.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Planning, medical readiness, patient participation and receiving-service availability remain distinct.
