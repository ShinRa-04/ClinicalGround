[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:U05:mission:01

**Original:** Provide specialist expertise to the designated reviewing decision-maker.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Insurer public material describes specialist expertise helping medical directors with complex cases.

**Alternatives:** Internal consultant, contracted expert or a separately appointed decision-maker; statutory external review is a distinct role.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The insurer page is undated and does not independently verify its current staffing/contracts. Consultant independence and final authority cannot be inferred from the name.
