## ROLE:G10:mission:01

**Original:** Resolve assigned health-information access and privacy questions.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The program identifies privacy-officer functions; HHS describes organization access/disclosure policies and purpose-dependent requirements.

**Alternatives:** HIM handling, designated privacy officer, legal advice and security/IAM support may be separate.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Minimum necessary, authorization, representative access and specially protected records are different questions; Part 2 illustrates why treatment is not an unrestricted all-records exception.
