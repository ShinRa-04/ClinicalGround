# D01 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D01:rule

**Original:** Patient participates and may request/refuse care; representative acts only under applicable authority.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE

**Reason:** Hospital patient participation/refusal and bounded representative authority are explicit. Separating consent from insurance/scheduling is a reasoned domain distinction.

**Alternatives:** Direct patient decision or a legally appropriate representative; specific exceptions require the actual law/case.

**Complexity / unresolved:** No universal surrogate order, capacity finding or emergency-consent outcome is established.
