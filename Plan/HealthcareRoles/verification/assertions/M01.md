## ROLE:M01:mission:01

**Original:** Coordinate a patient-centered plan and track unresolved needs.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Several accessible programs demonstrate assessment, shared planning, coordination and follow-through, with different staffing.

**Alternatives:** UCLA describes RN case managers; GRACE uses NP/social-work support teams; Partnership combines case managers, social workers and guides.

**Complexity / unresolved:** These examples do not establish national prevalence. Coordination title is not a license or independent prescribing credential; unavailable R23 is not needed to establish these examples.
