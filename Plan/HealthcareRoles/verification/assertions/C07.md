## ROLE:C07:mission:01

**Original:** Provide authorized practical nursing care and report patient status.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Practical nursing tasks, reporting and state-dependent permissions are explicit in the occupational description and scope framework.

**Alternatives:** LPN/LVN terminology and permitted procedures/supervision differ by jurisdiction and setting.

**Complexity / unresolved:** No national assertion is made about IV therapy, independent assessment or direct supervision in a particular case.
