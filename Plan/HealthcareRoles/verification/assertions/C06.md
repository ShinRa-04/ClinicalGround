# C06 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:C06:mission:01

**Original:** Assess nursing needs and provide/co-ordinate nursing care.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. RN assessment, treatment, teaching, observation and communication are supported as general duties; task permission is constrained separately.

**Alternatives:** Direct-care, UM and case-management RN functions differ despite the same underlying nursing credential.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Assignment is not evidence of legal delegation; additional APRN authority cannot be inferred from RN status alone.
