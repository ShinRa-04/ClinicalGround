## DECISION:D10:rule

**Original:** Membership/benefit information and request authorization are distinct findings.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_SOURCE_EXAMPLES

**Reason:** The sources distinguish benefits, authorization and financial responsibility.

**Alternatives:** Separate or combined staff can perform these checks; final claim/payment depends on its own applicable rules.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not infer final coverage or zero patient cost from eligibility, estimates or a case identifier.
