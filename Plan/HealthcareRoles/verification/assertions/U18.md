# U18 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:U18:mission:01

**Original:** Review an eligible dispute under the applicable external-review process.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The relevant external-review guidance supports an independent outcome binding on the insurer within its covered process.

**Alternatives:** State external review, HHS-administered route or qualifying IRO arrangement; eligibility and expedited handling differ.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not apply this route to every plan/denial, or substitute it for MA IRE review. Further legal remedies are not comprehensively audited here.
