## ROLE:G14:mission:01

**Original:** Administer assigned user-access controls and account problems.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. General IT administration supports access-permission work; HIPAA role/purpose controls support the healthcare constraint.

**Alternatives:** Dedicated IAM administrator, application support or another assigned access-control function.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Exact healthcare provisioning workflow, approval authority and local job title remain unconfirmed; administrator access is not unrestricted PHI permission.
