## ROLE:J03:mission:01

**Original:** Exercise judicial review when the statutory conditions are met.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Judicial review is identified as a later MA appeal stage, subject to actual legal requirements.

**Alternatives:** Only eligible properly pursued judicial cases; further appellate law is outside the described five-level administrative/initial judicial map.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The court is not an ordinary healthcare employee or a routine prerequisite to care.
