## ROLE:G04:mission:01

**Original:** Lead care-coordination operations and service improvement.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Programme leadership, resources, oversight and outcomes are described; the exact multilevel chart is organization-specific.

**Alternatives:** Several directors/managers or a combined lead in a smaller program.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Administrative resource authority does not confer prescribing, patient consent or insurer appeal authority.
