## ROLE:M29:mission:01

**Original:** Offer desired spiritual or existential support.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Provider team description and dated hospice composition guidance recognize spiritual/pastoral support.

**Alternatives:** Participation depends on wishes, needs and service setting; may be within or outside hospice.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Spiritual support does not create clinical, coverage or surrogate-decision authority.
