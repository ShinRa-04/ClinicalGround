# D03 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D03:rule

**Original:** Authorized treating practitioner makes clinical recommendations/orders within license and privileges.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL

**Reason:** Treating-professional functions and hospital order authentication are supported; profession/jurisdiction/privileges condition the permission.

**Alternatives:** Physician, NP, PA or other allowed practitioner for a specific activity/setting.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Sources do not establish any actual individual license, privileges or authorization; no universal prescribing permission is certified.
