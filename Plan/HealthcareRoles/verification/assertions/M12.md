## ROLE:M12:mission:01

**Original:** Assess and support nutrition-related needs within qualification.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Nutrition assessment, counseling, plans and monitoring are described; credential and practice permissions remain conditional.

**Alternatives:** Registered dietitian and other nutrition titles may have different credentials and permitted activities.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not assume every nutritionist title equals an RD credential or independently grants unrelated treatment authority.
