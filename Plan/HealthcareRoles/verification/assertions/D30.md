## DECISION:D30:rule

**Original:** Technical changes/access require authorized purpose and change permissions.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONTEXT_WITH_LOCAL_PERMISSION_UNKNOWN

**Reason:** IT permissions, healthcare access-purpose controls and hospital authentication are documented in their settings.

**Alternatives:** Technical administration can be assigned separately from clinical/policy authorship.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Actual system access is not proof of permission to alter clinical facts; exact change-control process remains local.
