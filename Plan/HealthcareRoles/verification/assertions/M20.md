## ROLE:M20:mission:01

**Original:** Coordinate receipt of the patient and information at the receiving facility.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Sources establish transfer to receiving facilities and information exchange, not one nationally standardized receiving-coordinator occupation.

**Alternatives:** Home health, skilled nursing, rehabilitation and other facilities have different intake and clinical assessment arrangements.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Reception/acceptance is not benefit approval or automatic authentication of all medical orders; exact local permissions are not established.
