## ROLE:M02:mission:01

**Original:** Assess psychosocial and practical support needs and connect appropriate resources.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Social assessment, resources, coping and coordination are supported. The prior label nonclinical would be too restrictive for all medical social workers.

**Alternatives:** Resource/social casework, clinically supervised pre-licensure practice, or separately licensed clinical work according to state and employer.

**Complexity / unresolved:** UCLA describes an unlicensed Clinical Social Worker II role. A job title containing clinical does not establish independent clinical licensure.
