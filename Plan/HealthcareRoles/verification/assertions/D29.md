# D29 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D29:rule

**Original:** Management may assign work within organizational mandate and lawful scope.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_FRAMEWORK_AND_SCOPED_BOUNDARY

**Reason:** Management, practice-scope guidance and reconsideration independence are separately evidenced.

**Alternatives:** Administrative reporting, professional supervision and statutory case review can follow different chains.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A local organogram is not supplied; no universal authority ordering between licenses or job titles is certified.
