## DECISION:D28:rule

**Original:** Communication/navigation functions support understanding and access.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETATION_WITH_PROFESSIONAL_CONTEXT

**Reason:** Communication/navigation functions are documented; no medical/coverage/proxy power is inferred merely from performing them.

**Alternatives:** One person may hold additional independent qualifications or appointment, which must be established separately.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The role boundary is not proof that an interpreter/navigator can never have another legitimate authority.
