# M16 assertion review

## ROLE:M16:mission:01

**Original:** Assess and treat relevant communication or swallowing difficulties.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Communication/swallowing evaluation, interventions, progress and family education are described.

**Alternatives:** Speech/language impairment support and language interpretation may both be needed but are different services.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Speech-language pathology does not make someone a qualified interpreter for every language or an unrelated medical prescriber.
