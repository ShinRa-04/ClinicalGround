## ROLE:O01:mission:01

**Original:** Move a supported request through the provider's authorization workflow.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Accessible provider descriptions support assembling relevant record information, tracking requests and appeals. Expired Mayo R03 is not needed for support.

**Alternatives:** Dedicated authorization specialist or a combined scheduling/referral position.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Requests, receipt, approval, scheduling and delivery are distinct events; title alone does not grant clinical decision authority.
