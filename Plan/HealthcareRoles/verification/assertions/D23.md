## DECISION:D23:rule

**Original:** 484.60 identifies physician/allowed-practitioner plan establishment/signature and scoped order/revision requirements.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE

**Reason:** The home-health provision distinguishes plan/signature, orders, permitted verbal orders and revisions.

**Alternatives:** Physician/allowed practitioner within applicable scope; agency coordination and clinical signatures are different roles.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not transplant hospice certification or another setting's signatory rules into home health.
