## ROLE:G11:mission:01

**Original:** Produce accurate authorized information about service and program performance.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Data validation, reporting, outcomes information and operational analysis are described.

**Alternatives:** Disease registry, population-health analysis or broader operational reporting functions.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Measure definitions, data access and interpretation must be authorized; administrative task completion cannot be assumed to mean clinical benefit.
