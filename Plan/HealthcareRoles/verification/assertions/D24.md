# D24 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D24:rule

**Original:** Referral sent, accepted, scheduled and completed are different events.

**Verdict:** OPERATIONAL_MODEL_NOT_EMPIRICAL_ASSERTION

**Reason:** Separating sent/accepted/scheduled/completed states is a proposed control informed by coordination needs, not a verified universal state machine.

**Alternatives:** Organizations use different status fields, acknowledgements and closure criteria.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The model cannot prove delivery or clinical benefit; actual evidence must be obtained in its local workflow.
