## ROLE:U15:mission:01

**Original:** Administer intake and record movement for the specified external-review route.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Federal external-review administration is described; a generic administrator profile is a functional model across different programs.

**Alternatives:** Different state/federal contractors and MA/commercial routes.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Administrative eligibility/file handling must not be confused with medical merits or universal agency jurisdiction.
