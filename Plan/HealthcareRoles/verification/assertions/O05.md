## ROLE:O05:mission:01

**Original:** Arrange feasible service timing, location and staff availability.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Scheduling duties and coordination with clinicians/services are directly described; readiness gates are modeled, not an observed universal protocol.

**Alternatives:** Standalone scheduler or combined referral/authorization worker.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A booking is not proof of clinical readiness, consent, authorization or service completion.
