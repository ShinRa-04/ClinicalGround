# M24 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:M24:mission:01

**Original:** Deliver the arranged transport within the assigned service.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The guide explicitly addresses transport providers/drivers and program assurances; exact pickup/reporting duties are operational examples, not national job requirements.

**Alternatives:** Different vehicle/accessibility and attendant qualifications; some services include separately qualified clinical personnel.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Driver title alone does not grant emergency clinical triage authority. A trip booking is not proof of attendance or completed care.
