## ROLE:O10:mission:01

**Original:** Organize the provider response to an adverse outcome.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The provider role supports authorization appeals and the professional article describes claims-denial work; the two must not be collapsed.

**Alternatives:** Pre-service coverage challenge, correction/peer discussion or downstream claim appeal as applicable.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Standing and representation need separate verification; a billing/appeal job title is not a patient appointment.
