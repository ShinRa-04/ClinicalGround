## ROLE:C04:mission:01

**Original:** Provide advanced nursing/clinical care within authorized practice.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Advanced practice duties are described generally; the California regulator gives concrete alternative practice categories and employer conditions.

**Alternatives:** California traditional standardized-procedure practice, 103 category and 104 statutory pathway differ; other states have different arrangements.

**Complexity / unresolved:** California page includes prospective/stale 104 availability wording. Its pathway description does not verify current certification of an individual or present application availability.

**Original:** Specify state, population focus, privileges and any required practice arrangement before concluding independent authority.

**Verdict:** SCOPED_CONDITION_OR_EXPLICIT_UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_FACT

**Reason:** Conditions are limitations on use, not assertions that an unidentified local case satisfies them. California page includes prospective/stale 104 availability wording. Its pathway description does not verify current certification of an individual or present application availability.

**Corrected wording:** Specify state, population focus, privileges and any required practice arrangement before concluding independent authority. California examples separate practice category, furnishing authority and employer requirements. Prospective 104-availability wording is not proof of present individual certification or application availability.

**Complexity / unresolved:** California page includes prospective/stale 104 availability wording. Its pathway description does not verify current certification of an individual or present application availability.
