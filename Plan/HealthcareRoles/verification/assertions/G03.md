## ROLE:G03:mission:01

**Original:** Review and approve the program's applicable utilization policies.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The MA policy-governance obligations are explicit; committee authority is bounded by governing criteria.

**Alternatives:** Different products and organizations have different committee structures and approval processes.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Policy approval is not adjudication of every case. An earlier rule edition may contain requirements removed or changed later; use the stated edition.
