## DECISION:D34:rule

**Original:** Oversight, consumer assistance and case appeal have distinct mandates.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONTEXT_WITH_MANDATE_LIMITS

**Reason:** Sources describe different assistance, complaint and appeal pathways; routing depends on the actual issue.

**Alternatives:** Insurer appeal, public counseling, quality complaint or oversight mechanism as applicable.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A complaint does not automatically establish an appeal, jurisdiction or reversal; the exact agency mandate remains case-specific.
