## ROLE:P02:mission:01

**Original:** Support the patient with practical help and observations.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Guidance recognizes people involved in care and caregiver education. The particular practical tasks are illustrative, not a verified mandatory family job.

**Alternatives:** Paid carers, family, friends, or no informal caregiver; patient wishes and lawful involvement control.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Being helpful or related does not by itself establish access to all records or substitute decision authority.
