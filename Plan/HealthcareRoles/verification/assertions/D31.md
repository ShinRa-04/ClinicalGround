# D31 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D31:rule

**Original:** Service staff coordinate fulfillment; qualified clinical input handles medical requirements.

**Verdict:** SERVICE_MODEL_WITH_SCOPED_SOURCE_CONTEXT

**Reason:** Equipment and Medicaid transport sources establish service-specific responsibilities; a general suitability workflow is a model.

**Alternatives:** Different equipment, transport administration, vehicle and attendant arrangements.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A generic driver/dispatcher/delivery title does not establish clinical qualification; actual orders/protocols are not provided.
