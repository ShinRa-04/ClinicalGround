## ROLE:G08:mission:01

**Original:** Maintain traceable policy analysis and draft operational requirements.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The source documents drafting, reviewing, tracking and maintaining policies and operational change support.

**Alternatives:** Clinical adviser and nonclinical policy analyst can be separate qualifications/functions.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Drafting and approval are distinct. The analyst does not automatically enact policy or decide clinical case merits.
