# D13 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D13:rule

**Original:** Under 422.590(h), reconsiderers were not involved in the initial decision; medical-necessity reconsideration is made by an appropriately expert physician.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE

**Reason:** Noninvolvement and physician expertise for the specified MA medical-necessity reconsideration are explicit.

**Alternatives:** Reviewer need not always have identical specialty; other products/types have their own requirements.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Higher rank does not by itself establish independence or case eligibility.
