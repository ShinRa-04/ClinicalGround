## ROLE:M15:mission:01

**Original:** Support safe performance of daily activities and relevant adaptations.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Daily-activity assessment, interventions, adaptations, equipment recommendations and training are described.

**Alternatives:** Different service settings and referral/order arrangements.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Equipment recommendation is not itself a covered equipment order, supplier acceptance or payment approval.
