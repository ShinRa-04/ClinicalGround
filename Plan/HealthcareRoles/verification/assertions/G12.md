## ROLE:G12:mission:01

**Original:** Translate operational needs into documented process/system changes.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The plan describes operational requirements, test/implementation plans and analytical support.
