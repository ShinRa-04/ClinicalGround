## ROLE:P07:mission:01

**Original:** Help route concerns and improve access to the appropriate resolution process.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The hospital example describes patient-concern handling; federal hospital grievance requirements support a route distinct from coverage appeals.

**Alternatives:** Patient relations, grievance office, navigator or other designated function; not always a separate advocate.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Patient advocacy title alone does not confer legal representation or power to reverse coverage.
