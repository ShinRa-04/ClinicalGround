## DECISION:D07:rule

**Original:** Assignment/delegation must fit applicable law, competence, supervision and policy.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_PROFESSIONAL_FRAMEWORK

**Reason:** The decision framework checks legal prohibition, education/competence, policy and accountability. It is professional guidance, not itself every state law.

**Alternatives:** Assignment, delegation and supervision vary by activity/state/credential.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Specific task permission remains unresolved until the actual practice act and case facts are checked.
