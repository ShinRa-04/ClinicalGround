**Original:** Peer discussion exchanges clinical information under the plan process.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_PROCESS_EXAMPLE_WITH_LIMITS

**Reason:** Payer material separately describes peer discussion and pre-service appeal. Deadline preservation is not established and is intentionally not assumed.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Retrieved deadline presentations differ. Do not select one as a universal rule or infer that discussion preserves filing rights.
