## ROLE:O08:mission:01

**Original:** Help clarify documentation needed to accurately represent the care provided.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The accessible professional-association article supports CDI/coding collaboration in denial management. It is dated and is not a current mandatory local procedure.

**Alternatives:** A dedicated CDI specialist or collaboration between clinicians and HIM/coding staff.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Clinical author authentication and permitted correction remain setting-specific; clarification is not permission to invent diagnoses.
