## DECISION:D27:rule

**Original:** Use purpose, applicable rule/authority and scope; privacy/HIM process resolves uncertain releases.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL_WITH_COMPLEXITY

**Reason:** HIPAA minimum-necessary exceptions, representative authority and specific Part 2 protections show that information-sharing permission depends on more than a treatment label.

**Alternatives:** Different purposes, record regimes and consent/exception pathways; applicable Part 2 TPO consent differs from special counseling notes.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The treatment exception is not an unrestricted all-records disclosure rule. No patient/program-specific legal determination is made.

**Original:** A10 lists minimum-necessary exceptions including specified treatment disclosures; other protections still apply.

**Verdict:** APPLICABILITY_CONDITION_OR_UNRESOLVED_CASE_FACT

**Reason:** HIPAA minimum-necessary exceptions, representative authority and specific Part 2 protections show that information-sharing permission depends on more than a treatment label.

**Corrected wording:** A10 lists minimum-necessary exceptions including specified treatment disclosures; other protections still apply. **Alternatives:** Different purposes, record regimes and consent/exception pathways; applicable Part 2 TPO consent differs from special counseling notes.
