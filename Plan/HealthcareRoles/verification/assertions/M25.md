## ROLE:M25:mission:01

**Original:** Arrange and document the ordered equipment service.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The CMS power-mobility example supports practitioner/supplier documentation, authorization, assessment and fulfillment distinctions.

**Alternatives:** Ordering professional or supplier can submit the specified request under the example; not both for that request.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Power-mobility requirements cannot be generalized to all DME, delivery staff or fitting credentials. Supplier is not automatically the prescriber.
