# D21 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D21:rule

**Original:** Lead coordinator integrates needs, goals and assigned follow-up with patient and responsible professionals.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_PROGRAM_MODELS_NOT_UNIVERSAL_AUTHORITY

**Reason:** Published programs support coordinating plans with patient and other professionals, with different staffing.

**Alternatives:** RN case management, NP/SW teams and mixed staff programs; underlying clinical authority stays credential-dependent.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Coordination ownership is not a verified national prescribing/coverage permission or a prevalence statement.
