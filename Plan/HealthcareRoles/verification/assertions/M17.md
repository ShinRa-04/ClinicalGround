## ROLE:M17:mission:01

**Original:** Provide assigned cardiopulmonary assessment, treatment and education.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Respiratory assessment, treatment, monitoring and equipment/medication education are described.

**Alternatives:** Authorized protocols and individual orders differ by setting and qualification.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The occupational summary does not establish a particular independent treatment-adjustment permission.
