## DECISION:D06:rule

**Original:** Apply the actual state and practice requirements for collaboration/supervision.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL_WITH_ALTERNATIVES

**Reason:** Oregon explicitly describes collaborative practice and associate terminology, limiting broad national supervision summaries.

**Alternatives:** Different states/practice arrangements; physician or employer agreement as specified by Oregon.

**Complexity / unresolved:** National occupational text is not a state practice act; no universal supervision ratio or boss chain is established.
