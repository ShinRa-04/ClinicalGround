# D25 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D25:rule

**Original:** Technician processes permitted tasks; pharmacist/prescriber provides the relevant professional judgment.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL_WITH_ALTERNATIVES

**Reason:** Professional task separation is supported; Washington illustrates separately authorized pharmacist therapy decisions.

**Alternatives:** Technician support, pharmacist professional decision and prescriber/protocol actions differ.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Not every pharmacist change requires a new individual physician instruction; no general independent technician therapy authority follows from title.
