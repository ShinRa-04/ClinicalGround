# C02 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:C02:mission:01

**Original:** Maintain clinical continuity and connect the patient to an appropriate service.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Primary-care and coordination sources support continuity/referral work. This is a function across several professions, not a new universal credential.

**Alternatives:** Physician, NP or PA may perform the function within their actual scope; the referrer and treating clinician may be the same person.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A referral is not insurance authorization; receiving-specialist input does not prove a universal reporting hierarchy.
