## ROLE:G02:mission:01

**Original:** Lead the assigned payer clinical review program.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The MA committee rule names medical-director leadership; insurer material illustrates program/case functions.

**Alternatives:** Clinical-program oversight and separately delegated individual review can be combined or separated.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Program leadership cannot substitute for the independence/expertise required for a particular appeal.
