## ROLE:P06:mission:01

**Original:** Render written information accurately in another language.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Written translation is distinct from interpretation; fidelity of content is supported. Escalation of ambiguous wording is a proposed quality-control action.

**Alternatives:** One worker may perform both functions if qualified, but the skills remain distinct.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Producing a translation does not demonstrate comprehension or valid consent.
