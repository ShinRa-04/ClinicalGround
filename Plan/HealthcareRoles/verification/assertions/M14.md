## ROLE:M14:mission:01

**Original:** Assess and improve movement/function within physical-therapy practice.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Functional evaluation, therapy planning/interventions, monitoring and education are described.

**Alternatives:** Referral/direct-access and payer requirements can differ; a therapist does not necessarily sit in a universal physician command chain.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Professional clinical assessment and authorization/payment criteria are separate. Actual state/order rules are not nationally resolved.
