## DECISION:D18:rule

**Original:** Coverage, professional treatment judgment and patient willingness are separate domains.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETATION_OF_DISTINCT_AUTHORITIES

**Reason:** Different source provisions address participation, clinical care and benefits. The domain separation is a reasoned synthesis rather than one quoted universal hierarchy statute.

**Alternatives:** Clinical recommendations and coverage/consent outcomes may agree or conflict and require their own processes.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The reference does not decide a real individual case or eliminate any actual legal constraint.
