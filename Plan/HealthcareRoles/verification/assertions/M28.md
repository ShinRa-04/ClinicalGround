## ROLE:M28:mission:01

**Original:** Coordinate the applicable hospice plan and patient/family support.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Official 2025 codification provides stronger evidence than the earlier 2024 snippet: IDG, designated RN coordination, physician certification and admission functions are distinct.

**Corrected wording:** Coordinate hospice care while distinguishing interdisciplinary, RN coordination, attending and certifying/admission functions.

**Alternatives:** A patient-designated attending may be physician/NP/PA under the benefit description, but that does not make all of them authorized terminal-illness certifiers.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The IDG is a group, not one individual. Evidence is expressly the October 2025 codification; complete current 2026 hospice-rule verification is not claimed.
