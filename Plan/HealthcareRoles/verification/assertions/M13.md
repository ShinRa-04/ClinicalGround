# M13 assertion review

## ROLE:M13:mission:01

**Original:** Help people understand and implement relevant health-management skills.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Educational needs, materials, teaching and evaluation are documented; coach is a broader functional title.

**Alternatives:** Health educator, program coach, CHW or licensed clinician providing an educational function.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Providing education is not by itself a medical diagnosis or prescribing grant.
