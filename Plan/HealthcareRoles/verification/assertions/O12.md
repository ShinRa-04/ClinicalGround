## ROLE:O12:mission:01

**Original:** Manage the provider operation and resolve work-routing problems.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Health-service management descriptions support operational staffing and supervision; exact escalation routing is a model.

**Alternatives:** Practice management, centralized patient access or other assigned supervisory post.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Operational reporting rank does not establish clinical license or insurer signoff authority.
