## DECISION:D26:rule

**Original:** Use actual license and separately granted permissions.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL_WITH_ALTERNATIVES

**Reason:** New Mexico provides a concrete additional certification route, while general clinical psychology duties are separately described.

**Alternatives:** Ordinary psychology license and additional conditional/full prescribing qualification are distinct.

**Complexity / unresolved:** No universal psychologist prescribing rule or verified permission for an individual is established.
