## ROLE:P04:mission:01

**Original:** Pursue a coverage dispute within the appointment and applicable appeal route.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Appointment and scope requirements are directly documented in the Medicare representation guidance; provider benefits assignment is distinct.

**Alternatives:** Self-representation, appointed lay representative or qualified professional under the actual route.

**Complexity / unresolved:** An appeal appointment does not establish treatment proxy authority; filing/standing details differ by product.
