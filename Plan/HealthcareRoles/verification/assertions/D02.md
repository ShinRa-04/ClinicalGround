# D02 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D02:rule

**Original:** Recognize authority from applicable law/instrument/appointment and its actual scope, not family relationship alone.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL

**Reason:** HHS defines healthcare representation and separately describes appointment for Medicare appeals.

**Alternatives:** Broad or limited healthcare authority; appointed appeal representative; statutory authority where applicable.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Two scopes can belong to the same person, but neither follows merely from family relationship or a generic signature.
