## ROLE:M23:mission:01

**Original:** Arrange the practical transport needed for an agreed journey.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Medicaid guidance supports several transport-administration models. The detailed dispatch workflow remains a proposed service interface.

**Alternatives:** Brokered, non-broker and other permissible state arrangements; emergency and non-emergency services differ.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Travel eligibility, accessibility, modality and required clinical attendants cannot be decided from dispatcher title alone.
