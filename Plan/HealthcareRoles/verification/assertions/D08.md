## DECISION:D08:rule

**Original:** Responsible service author authenticates clinical content; custodians transfer and CDI requests clarification.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SETTING_PLUS_INTERPRETATION

**Reason:** Hospital authentication is explicit; coding/CDI sources support clarification rather than independently creating clinical facts.

**Alternatives:** Responsible author and permitted countersign/correction arrangements depend on setting.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The hospital rule is not silently generalized to all clinics or used as a complete record-correction policy.
