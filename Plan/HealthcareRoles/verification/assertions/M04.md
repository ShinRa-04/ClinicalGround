# M04 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:M04:mission:01

**Original:** Support safe and effective medication use.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Medication review, counseling and professional communication are general duties. Washington provides a concrete delegated drug-therapy pathway.

**Alternatives:** Dispensing/counseling, clinical recommendations, and separately authorized therapy modification/initiation are different activities.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not imply every change requires a fresh individual physician instruction or every pharmacist can independently prescribe; actual legal/program authority controls.

**Original:** Professional scope, collaborative arrangements and specific state permissions must be identified for therapy changes.

**Verdict:** SCOPED_CONDITION_OR_EXPLICIT_UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_FACT

**Reason:** Conditions are limitations on use, not assertions that an unidentified local case satisfies them. Do not imply every change requires a fresh individual physician instruction or every pharmacist can independently prescribe; actual legal/program authority controls.

**Corrected wording:** Professional scope, collaborative arrangements and specific state permissions must be identified for therapy changes. Washington CDTA is one documented pathway for specified therapy activity; no universal requirement for a new physician instruction on each authorized action is inferred.

**Alternatives:** Dispensing/counseling, clinical recommendations, and separately authorized therapy modification/initiation are different activities.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not imply every change requires a fresh individual physician instruction or every pharmacist can independently prescribe; actual legal/program authority controls.
