## ROLE:U11:mission:01

**Original:** Help requesting organizations navigate operational payer processes.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Provider-facing operational contact is documented, while the precise staff task allocation is a synthesized functional description.

**Alternatives:** Provider services, UM intake or a delegated organization may be the contact.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The correct counterparty and what it can decide must be confirmed for the actual case.
