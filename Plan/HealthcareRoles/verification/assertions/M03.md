## ROLE:M03:mission:01

**Original:** Provide qualified clinical social-work assessment and treatment alongside coordination.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The description supports licensed clinical social-work assessment/treatment while distinguishing supervised/pre-licensure pathways.

**Alternatives:** Independent or supervised clinical work under the actual license/setting; case management and psychotherapy are not interchangeable functions.

**Complexity / unresolved:** State license/permissions remain necessary; the profile does not establish medication-prescribing or coverage authority.

- [B09](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/social-workers.htm) — tool line 242; access: tool_excerpt. Passage anchor: `licensed to diagnose`.
