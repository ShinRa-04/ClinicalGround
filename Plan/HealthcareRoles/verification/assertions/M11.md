## ROLE:M11:mission:01

**Original:** Help members connect with resources and coordinate practical benefit access.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The plan describes guides linking members/families/providers/agencies and resources.

**Alternatives:** Different guide levels and discretion within the stated program; other organizations use different titles or staff.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The title and hierarchy cannot be transferred nationally or treated as equivalent to licensed clinical case management.
