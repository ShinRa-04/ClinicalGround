## ROLE:C08:mission:01

**Original:** Support clinic operations and delegated clinical/administrative tasks.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Administrative support and permitted directed clinical tasks are described; this is explicitly distinct from PA clinical practice.

**Alternatives:** Administrative-focused, clinical-focused or mixed assignments; permissible tasks differ by state/employer.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Job assignment or training alone cannot establish every clinical permission.
