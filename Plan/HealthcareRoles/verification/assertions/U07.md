## ROLE:U07:mission:01

**Original:** Evaluate medication evidence and pharmacy-program requirements.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The professional description supports medication-program review; the program example permits pharmacy-specific professional denial review.

**Alternatives:** Pharmacist review/recommendation, protocol-based approval, or additional physician review according to the product.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not apply a medical-service Part C appeal rule to every drug benefit. Coverage review and prescribing remain different authorities.

## ROLE:U07:condition:01

**Original:** Medication pathways can differ from medical-service prior authorization and Part C item/service appeals.

**Verdict:** SCOPED_CONDITION_OR_EXPLICIT_UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_FACT

**Reason:** Conditions are limitations on use, not assertions that an unidentified local case satisfies them. Do not apply a medical-service Part C appeal rule to every drug benefit. Coverage review and prescribing remain different authorities.

**Corrected wording:** Medication pathways can differ from medical-service prior authorization and Part C item/service appeals. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.

**Alternatives:** Pharmacist review/recommendation, protocol-based approval, or additional physician review according to the product.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not apply a medical-service Part C appeal rule to every drug benefit. Coverage review and prescribing remain different authorities.
