## ROLE:U09:mission:01

**Original:** Deliver the authorized outcome with accurate procedural information.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Program duties and notice content are explicit. A notification task is not identical to making the substantive clinical decision.

**Alternatives:** Notices may be sent through different permitted media and to different required recipients.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Receipt, decision, dispatch and implementation are separate events. Actual timing/contents must be resolved for the route.
