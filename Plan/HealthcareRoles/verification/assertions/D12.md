[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D12:rule

**Original:** Before issuance, 422.566(d) requires a physician OR other appropriate licensed healthcare professional with relevant expertise and Medicare-criteria knowledge.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE

**Reason:** The retrieved section directly requires appropriate physician or other qualified professional review before the specified adverse MA medical-necessity determination.

**Alternatives:** Physician or another appropriate licensed expert; organization policy can set additional requirements.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Integrated-plan and decision-type conditions matter; the rule is not physician-only for every initial denial.
