# G01 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:G01:mission:01

**Original:** Provide assigned clinical-program leadership in the provider organization.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Health-service management and medical-staff governance support a clinical-leadership context, not a single universal CMO job description.

**Alternatives:** Clinical-program director, medical-staff leadership and operational executive can be distinct posts.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Exact authority over practitioners requires bylaws, privileges and assignment; the title does not establish a physician-over-all-professions chain.
