## ROLE:M08:mission:01

**Original:** Provide the counseling and recovery support permitted by the actual qualification.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Counseling assessment, plans, progress and referrals are described, but credentials within this broad group differ.

**Alternatives:** Mental-health, addiction, marriage/family and other counseling roles; supervised or independent practice depends on applicable qualification.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not infer equivalent clinical scope or prescribing authority across counseling titles.
