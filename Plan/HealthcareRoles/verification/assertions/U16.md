# U16 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:U16:mission:01

**Original:** Help people understand rights and find the appropriate assistance channel.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The source identifies Medicare help/counseling and complaint contacts; each has a different mandate.

**Alternatives:** SHIP counseling, insurer assistance, quality-of-care complaint support or another appropriate ombudsman route.

**Complexity / unresolved:** These are several functions, not one credential or one organization that can reverse every denial.
