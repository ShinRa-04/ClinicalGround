## ROLE:U13:mission:01

**Original:** Make the applicable reconsideration judgment independently of the initial decision-making.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The specified MA reconsideration requires prior noninvolvement and appropriate physician expertise for medical-necessity denial.

**Alternatives:** The professional need not always match the treating specialty; non-medical-necessity and other product procedures can differ.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Independence is not merely seniority. This card does not establish the credentials/delegation of any actual reviewer.
