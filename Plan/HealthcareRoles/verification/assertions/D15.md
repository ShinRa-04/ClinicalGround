## DECISION:D15:rule

**Original:** CMS-contracted independent entity resolves remaining disputed issues; reversal triggers scoped effectuation obligations.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE

**Reason:** Independent MA review and scoped reversal effectuation are explicit.

**Alternatives:** Outcome may affirm, reverse, dismiss/remand where allowed; implementation depends on decision category and further-review conditions.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A favorable decision and actual authorization/provision/payment are distinct; no universal deadline is supplied.
