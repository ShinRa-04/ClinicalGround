## DECISION:D17:rule

**Original:** CMS lists plan reconsideration, IRE, adjudication, Council and district-court review as successive appeal levels.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE

**Reason:** The MA overview names successive levels, while the effectuation rule provides a Council-review exception.

**Alternatives:** Further appeal is conditional on actual standing/jurisdiction/filing; reversals can have implementation conditions.

**Complexity / unresolved:** This is not an employment ladder or a guarantee every case may reach every forum; no threshold amounts are invented.
