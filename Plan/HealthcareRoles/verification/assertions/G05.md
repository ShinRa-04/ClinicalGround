## ROLE:G05:mission:01

**Original:** Supervise assigned staff and support complex case coordination.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The source explicitly distinguishes RN, LVN, social-work and nonclinical supervision with scope limits.

**Alternatives:** Administrative supervision and discipline-specific clinical supervision may be different relationships.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A supervisor title does not imply identical clinical powers or a universal ordering between professional licenses.
