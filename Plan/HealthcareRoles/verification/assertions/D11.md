## DECISION:D11:rule

**Original:** A reviewer may approve only under documented delegation, competence and applicable criteria.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_ORGANIZATION_EXAMPLES_WITH_CORRECTION

**Reason:** The program allows limited nonclinical approvals under explicit criteria as well as licensed review; the earlier clinical-only emphasis was incomplete.

**Corrected wording:** Published programs may permit limited nonclinical approvals under explicit criteria and assigned benefit decisions, alongside licensed clinical review and escalation; authority is delegation- and product-specific.

**Alternatives:** Nonclinical limited approval, benefit-only decision, licensed approval, or clinical escalation depending on documented delegation.

**Complexity / unresolved:** R13 also describes medical-director final responsibility and broader professional categories; exact signing rights require program/product clarification.
