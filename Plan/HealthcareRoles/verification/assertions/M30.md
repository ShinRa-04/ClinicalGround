## ROLE:M30:mission:01

**Original:** Keep assigned coordination logistics and communications organized.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Administrative coordination, referral distribution, data entry and program support are described.

**Alternatives:** Separate administrative coordinator or a person combining tasks with another role.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Administrative closure/status changes are not equivalent to clinical decision or confirmed service delivery.

## ROLE:M30:inputs:01

**Original:** Assigned coordination plan

**Verdict:** MODEL_NOT_CONFIRMED_EMPIRICAL_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_FACT

**Reason:** This is an explicit proposed field, contact, service boundary, condition or workflow-control choice. Sources inform its context but do not establish the exact design as actual or mandatory practice.

**Alternatives:** Local forms, contacts, staffing combinations and procedures may differ; the model does not select an institution-specific one.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Actual institution procedure, delegation, acknowledgement and closure rules are not supplied and remain unconfirmed.
