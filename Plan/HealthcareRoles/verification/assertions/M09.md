## ROLE:M09:mission:01

**Original:** Connect people and communities with accessible health and social support.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Community linkage, outreach, navigation and practical barrier support are described.

**Alternatives:** Community health representative, promotor, navigator or another program-specific title can overlap.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Community trust and additional training do not by themselves establish a clinical license or benefit-decision authority.
