# M22 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:M22:mission:01

**Original:** Deliver the assigned practical support or resource assessment.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Health/social resource connections are supported, but the food/housing/benefit worker grouping is a functional model rather than one verified profession.

**Alternatives:** Different agencies and eligibility/capacity rules; referral may be accepted, wait-listed or declined.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Directory availability is not service capacity, and the worker cannot be assumed to control health-plan eligibility or clinical treatment.
