## DECISION:D19:rule

**Original:** 422.137 requires UM committee review/approval of MA UM policies and documented decisions within governing criteria.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE

**Reason:** The MA committee approval/documentation and criteria constraints are explicit.

**Alternatives:** Other products may use different governance; analyst drafting and institutional approval are different functions.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Policy approval does not itself adjudicate all individual case disputes.
