# O13 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:O13:mission:01

**Original:** Supply authentic reports relevant to the clinical question.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Clinical report authors and record custodians have distinct evidenced functions. The combined profile is a modeling convenience, not one verified profession.

**Alternatives:** Interpreting physician, diagnostic professional, therapist or records custodian depending on the evidence.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The report author and person transmitting it may be different people with different permissions.
