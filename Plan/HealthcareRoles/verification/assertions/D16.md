# D16 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D16:rule

**Original:** In the applicable external-review process described by HealthCare.gov, insurer must accept the external decision.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL_WITH_ALTERNATIVES

**Reason:** Guidance supports binding acceptance within its applicable external-review route and identifies product/process limits.

**Alternatives:** State/federal qualifying routes; eligible urgent internal/external requests may run concurrently.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The prior simple linear diagram was incomplete for urgent cases. MA IRE and this external process must remain distinct.

## DECISION:D16:condition

**Original:** Determine product, eligibility, issue and route first.

**Verdict:** APPLICABILITY_CONDITION_OR_UNRESOLVED_CASE_FACT

**Reason:** Guidance supports binding acceptance within its applicable external-review route and identifies product/process limits.

**Corrected wording:** Determine product, eligibility, issue and route first. Check the applicable ACA/non-grandfathered context and actual external-review eligibility. Eligible urgent external review may be requested concurrently with internal appeal.

**Alternatives:** State/federal qualifying routes; eligible urgent internal/external requests may run concurrently.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The prior simple linear diagram was incomplete for urgent cases. MA IRE and this external process must remain distinct.
