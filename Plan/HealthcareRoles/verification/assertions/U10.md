# U10 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:U10:mission:01

**Original:** Help the patient navigate benefits, status and the right contact.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Public help channels and plan member-services functions support explanation/navigation, but the detailed contact workflow is not independently established.

**Alternatives:** Member services, clinical case team, dedicated appeals staff or public assistance channel may handle different questions.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Access to a status screen does not establish authority to decide merits; no universal call-center delegation is confirmed.
