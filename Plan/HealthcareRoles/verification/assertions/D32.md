# D32 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## DECISION:D32:rule

**Original:** Actual assistance administrator/payer adjudication decides under its program; counselors explain and help applications.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONTEXT_NOT_PROGRAM_SIGNOFF

**Reason:** Cost and assistance support are documented, but the actual administrator's approval authority depends on the named program.

**Alternatives:** Counseling, assistance decisions, claims and coverage are separate, possibly held by different organizations.

**Complexity / unresolved:** No assistance eligibility outcome, final payment guarantee or universal financial-decision hierarchy is established.
