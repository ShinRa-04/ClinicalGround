## ROLE:G17:mission:01

**Original:** Provide the legal advice or representation actually authorized for the issue.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Legal advice, analysis and authorized submissions are general lawyer duties; healthcare representation also requires the applicable appointment/standing.

**Alternatives:** Self/lay representation may be permitted in a process; not every appeal requires a lawyer.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Retainer/appointment and professional jurisdiction matter; advice does not itself reverse coverage or grant treatment permission.
