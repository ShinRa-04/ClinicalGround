## DECISION:D05:rule

**Original:** Activity depends on state scope, population focus and privileges/practice arrangement.

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED_CONDITIONAL_WITH_ALTERNATIVES

**Reason:** The occupational summary and California regulator establish variation and multiple arrangements.

**Alternatives:** California traditional/103/104 pathways have different conditions; furnishing authority and employer requirements are additional issues.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The California page has prospective 104 availability wording that does not itself prove current individual certification or operational application availability.
