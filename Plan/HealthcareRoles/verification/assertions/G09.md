## ROLE:G09:mission:01

**Original:** Support adherence to applicable program and organizational requirements.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Compliance monitoring and operational interpretation are documented management functions; legal/enforcement authority remains separately scoped.

**Alternatives:** Internal compliance staff, legal counsel and external regulator have different roles.

**Complexity / unresolved:** An internal interpretation is not itself law or a clinical/appeal decision.
