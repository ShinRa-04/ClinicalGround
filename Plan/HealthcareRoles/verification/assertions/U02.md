## ROLE:U02:mission:01

**Original:** Apply the plan's administrative membership and benefit information.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Administrative benefit evaluation and assigned benefit-only decisions are explicit in the program.

**Alternatives:** Benefit verification without decision power, or delegated administrative approval/denial, depending on the assignment.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A benefits title alone does not establish signatory power. Administrative coverage exclusions and clinical medical necessity need different reasoning/review.

## ROLE:U02:condition:01

**Original:** Exact benefit authority and decision notice are plan-specific; not every benefits specialist is authorized to issue a denial.

**Verdict:** SCOPED_CONDITION_OR_EXPLICIT_UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_FACT

**Reason:** Conditions are limitations on use, not assertions that an unidentified local case satisfies them. A benefits title alone does not establish signatory power. Administrative coverage exclusions and clinical medical necessity need different reasoning/review.

**Corrected wording:** Exact benefit authority and decision notice are plan-specific; not every benefits specialist is authorized to issue a denial. R13 permits some assigned benefit-only decisions; actual signatory authority still requires the plan/delegation.

**Alternatives:** Benefit verification without decision power, or delegated administrative approval/denial, depending on the assignment.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A benefits title alone does not establish signatory power. Administrative coverage exclusions and clinical medical necessity need different reasoning/review.
