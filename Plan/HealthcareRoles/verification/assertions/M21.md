## ROLE:M21:mission:01

**Original:** Provide assigned personal support and report relevant changes.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Personal support, recorded observations and supervision are described; permitted health-related tasks vary.

**Alternatives:** Home-health aide, other personal-care worker or additional trained support with distinct requirements.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not apply Medicare coverage conditions to every private aide or treat personal care as equivalent to skilled nursing.

## ROLE:M21:inputs:01

**Original:** Assigned plan and instructions

**Verdict:** MODEL_NOT_CONFIRMED_EMPIRICAL_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_FACT

**Reason:** This is an explicit proposed field, contact, service boundary, condition or workflow-control choice. Sources inform its context but do not establish the exact design as actual or mandatory practice.
