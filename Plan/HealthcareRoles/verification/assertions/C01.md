## ROLE:C01:mission:01

**Original:** Assess the clinical problem and recommend or order appropriate care.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. History/examination, tests, documentation, treatment recommendations and patient questions appear in physician duties; authentication is setting-specific.

**Alternatives:** Ordering and treating roles may coincide; another authorized profession may order permitted services.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Clinical recommendation, patient participation and coverage are separate functions; privileges and jurisdiction remain local facts.
