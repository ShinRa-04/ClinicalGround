## ROLE:M31:mission:01

**Original:** Handle a referred protection concern within the relevant service's mandate.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Sources establish protection-related referrals and an adult-protective-services policy context; no universal national safeguarding profession/power is established.

**Alternatives:** Adult APS, child protection, clinical social work and other services have different mandates and reporting law.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The 2024 federal APS rule fact sheet has a later full-compliance date. Do not infer uniform implementation or identical consent/investigation authority in every state.
