## DECISION:D20:rule

**Original:** Policy approval and case adjudication are different functions.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETATION_WITH_SCOPED_RULES

**Reason:** Sources assign different policy-governance and case-review functions. No committee command over the independent entity is evidenced.

**Alternatives:** Systemic policy review and an individual appeal can both be pursued through their own authorized mechanisms.

**Complexity / unresolved:** The absence of a command edge is a conservative modeling choice, not a claim to have audited every possible internal organogram.
