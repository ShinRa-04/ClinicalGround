## ROLE:M19:mission:01

**Original:** Coordinate acceptance and services for a home-health plan.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Home-health acceptance, coordinated disciplines and physician/allowed-practitioner plan/order responsibilities are described.

**Alternatives:** Agency intake and qualified clinical coordination can be separate functions; allowable signatories depend on setting and scope.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Agency capacity, clinical orders, accepted referral and completed visits are separate. Local intake/acceptance permissions remain unconfirmed.
