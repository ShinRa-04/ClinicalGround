## ROLE:M18:mission:01

**Original:** Coordinate a feasible transition and transfer necessary information.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The cited hospital rule directly supports needs/access evaluation, patient discussion, qualified planning and transfer information.

**Alternatives:** RN, social worker or other appropriately qualified personnel may develop or supervise the plan; exact local roles differ.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Medical readiness, discharge planning, receiving acceptance, coverage and patient rights are distinct; planner title is not sole authority.
