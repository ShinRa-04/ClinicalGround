# U04 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:U04:mission:01

**Original:** Perform assigned clinical coverage review and document the rationale.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The MA requirement supports qualified initial review, while insurer material illustrates physician case-review functions.

**Alternatives:** MA initial review may be by another appropriate licensed professional; a program may impose medical-director signoff beyond that minimum.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Initial review, final institutional accountability and independent reconsideration are separate; medical director is not a universal superior over the treating clinician.

## ROLE:U04:condition:01

**Original:** Case-review function is distinct from organization-wide CMO governance; authority depends on product, license, expertise and delegation.

**Verdict:** SCOPED_CONDITION_OR_EXPLICIT_UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_FACT

**Reason:** Conditions are limitations on use, not assertions that an unidentified local case satisfies them. Initial review, final institutional accountability and independent reconsideration are separate; medical director is not a universal superior over the treating clinician.

**Corrected wording:** Case-review function is distinct from organization-wide CMO governance; authority depends on product, license, expertise and delegation. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.

**Alternatives:** MA initial review may be by another appropriate licensed professional; a program may impose medical-director signoff beyond that minimum.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Initial review, final institutional accountability and independent reconsideration are separate; medical director is not a universal superior over the treating clinician.
