## ROLE:J02:mission:01

**Original:** Review the applicable appealed adjudicative decision under Council jurisdiction.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Council review is a named MA level and the effectuation rule contains a Council-review exception.

**Alternatives:** Further review can be sought under its actual rules; a plan request can affect implementation as described in A16.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Do not infer automatic review entitlement or that every earlier outcome is immediately final/effectuated.
