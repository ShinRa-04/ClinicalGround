## ROLE:C05:mission:01

**Original:** Provide clinical assessment and treatment within the applicable PA practice arrangement.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Clinical PA duties have general support. Oregon demonstrates a collaboration framework and associate terminology, preventing a universal supervision inference.

**Alternatives:** State-specific supervision/collaboration; Oregon requires a written agreement with physician or employer as described by its board.

**Complexity / unresolved:** National occupational summaries are not substitutes for state practice law. Physician assistant/associate terminology itself varies.
