## ROLE:P01:mission:01

**Original:** Express personal goals and make applicable informed choices about participation and care.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Patient participation and informed choices are directly described; reporting history/barriers is coordination context rather than a universal mandatory patient duty.

**Alternatives:** Patient may participate directly or through a lawful representative where applicable.

**Complexity / unresolved:** Patient rights do not create entitlement to inappropriate treatment; capacity, minors and emergency rules require case-specific law.
