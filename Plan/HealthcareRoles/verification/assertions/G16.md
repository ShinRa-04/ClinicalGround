## ROLE:G16:mission:01

**Original:** Exercise the specific public oversight authority assigned by law.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. Sources establish particular oversight/help routes, not an all-purpose regulator with every power listed by generic title.

**Alternatives:** CMS, state program, insurance regulator, quality oversight and ombudsman mandates differ.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A complaint may trigger oversight or referral rather than a coverage reversal; identify jurisdiction and legal process.
