# U06 assertion review

[Audit index](../README.md)

## ROLE:U06:mission:01

**Original:** Review behavioral-health service requests within the assigned professional scope.

**Verdict:** INTERPRETIVE_FUNCTION_SUMMARY

**Reason:** The mission summarizes a modeled function from the evidence and does not create an official occupation or grant authority. The program documents behavioral-health oversight and service-appropriate qualified review. Oversight and individual case decision authority must be distinguished.

**Alternatives:** Different qualified professions may contribute depending on service, state and delegation.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A designated behavioral program leader is not automatically the reviewer of every case; counselor, psychologist and physician qualifications are not interchangeable.

## ROLE:U06:condition:01

**Original:** Psychiatrist, psychologist and counselor credentials are not interchangeable; apply actual product and review requirements.

**Verdict:** SCOPED_CONDITION_OR_EXPLICIT_UNRESOLVED_LOCAL_FACT

**Reason:** Conditions are limitations on use, not assertions that an unidentified local case satisfies them. A designated behavioral program leader is not automatically the reviewer of every case; counselor, psychologist and physician qualifications are not interchangeable.

**Corrected wording:** Psychiatrist, psychologist and counselor credentials are not interchangeable; apply actual product and review requirements. R13 has both a wider professional-category list and medical-director final-responsibility language. Specific case review/signoff must be clarified, not inferred from either statement alone.

**Alternatives:** Different qualified professions may contribute depending on service, state and delegation.

**Complexity / unresolved:** A designated behavioral program leader is not automatically the reviewer of every case; counselor, psychologist and physician qualifications are not interchangeable.
