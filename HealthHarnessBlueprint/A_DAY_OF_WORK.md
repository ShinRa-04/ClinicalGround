# A hypothetical day of work

This illustrates the working organization, not a new clinical world or an actual χ-Bench run. The cases, work-item IDs and sequence below are invented examples. Clinical facts and outcomes would come from the supplied environment; this document does not supply them.

## The work board

| Work item | Assigned desk | Current supplied situation | Immediate responsibility | Working context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EX-A | Provider PA | Packet work is assigned and one requirement is unclear | PA owner uses requirement/applicability/evidence roles as needed | Patient A/task A only |
| EX-B | Payer UM | The task begins at a review stage with existing intake material | UM owner uses the relevant policy/evidence/review/authority functions | Patient B/task B only |
| EX-C | CM | Actual outreach/assessment work remains | CM owner manages actual dialogue, answers, goals and plan obligations | Patient C/task C only |

The board contains scoped work references and routing status. It is not a global patient chart injected into every staff member's prompt. The scheduler may coordinate capacity without exposing another patient's clinical history to the current worker.

## What the staff actually do

For EX-A, the composite PA specialist receives the task. It can perform several micro-responsibilities itself, or request a bounded policy/evidence helper. If a fact is missing, it uses the permitted source/interface or records the gap. It cannot generate a new clinician fact because a packet would be more convenient with one. Actual submission/response evidence governs later work.

For EX-B, staff reuse the same approved general role/procedure definitions but start from B's isolated inputs. Nothing learned about A's service, preferences, missing records or outcome enters B's context. The current stage can make some earlier roles unnecessary; an existing intake record should not be recreated solely to make the internal organization look busy.

For EX-C, the conversation responsibility preserves questions already asked and actual replies. A goal-elicitation function does not answer for the patient. A plan writer can organize sourced contributions without claiming that a referral or appointment already delivered care. When new information arrives, the case-local reconciliation role updates the affected obligations and source versions.

## Coordination without a master decision-maker

The operations coordinator tracks an unowned task or waiting response and routes it appropriately. It does not gain the patient's consent authority or the payer's determination authority. A specialist's recommendation remains a recommendation until the actual assigned decision/action occurs. A quality critic can flag a missing source; it is not a physician reconsiderer or the official grader.

The composite job owns continuity and final truthful disposition. If a worker is replaced or its context is reduced, the handoff contains the current request, scope, evidence references, unknowns and action receipts. An uncertain write is reconciled through the actual tool contract before any retry. A late result against an old snapshot cannot silently overwrite the newer case state.

## End-of-work receipts

The owner reports the assigned task's observed outcome and any remaining obligation/owner. Work can end in a supported exception or pending external state where the actual contract permits; it should not fabricate success because the day or model budget ended. Whether the benchmark scores that outcome as a pass remains a separate post-run evaluation.

Patient-specific working material stays in its permitted case/episode boundary. The next patient's job can reuse the staff definition and approved general knowledge, but not another patient's conversation, summary, failed attempt, inferred heuristic or learned habit.
